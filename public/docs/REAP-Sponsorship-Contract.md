### 1. Sport Waikato's Obligations

### 2. Sponsor's Obligations

2.1 **Payment.** Sponsor will pay the Sponsorship Fee of NZD $[amount] by [date]. Payment is due in full before the Season commences. No sponsorship benefits will be delivered until payment is confirmed.

2.2 **Brand assets.** Sponsor will provide Sport Waikato with approved logo files, brand guidelines, and any other required assets at least 21 days before the Season commencement date.

2.3 **Compliance.** Sponsor will not use its association with REAP to make any claim about participant health outcomes, gambling, prize eligibility, or the nature of REAP's subscription in a way that contradicts Sport Waikato's published Terms, Rules, or legal position.

2.4 **Confidentiality.** Sponsor will treat any participant data (including aggregated data provided in reports) as confidential. Reports may be used internally for health and wellness programme evaluation only. They may not be shared with third parties or published without Sport Waikato's written consent.

---

### 3. Data Access and Privacy

3.1 **No individual data.** Sport Waikato will not provide Sponsor with individual participant data under this Agreement. Individual data is only available where participants have separately and specifically opted in to data sharing with the named Sponsor (see 3.2).

3.2 **Individual opt-in (if applicable).** Where Sponsor wishes to contact or offer benefits to individual participants, a separate opt-in mechanism will be made available to participants in the REAP app. This opt-in:
- Names the Sponsor specifically
- Describes exactly what data will be shared and for what purpose
- Is entirely voluntary and does not affect REAP participation
- Can be revoked by the participant at any time

Sport Waikato will not include sponsor data-sharing consent in the general Terms of Participation. It will be presented as a separate, standalone choice.

3.3 **Group report minimum size.** Aggregated group reports will not be produced for groups of fewer than 10 participants. For groups of 10–20, data will be presented in ranges rather than precise figures to reduce re-identification risk.

3.4 **Privacy Act compliance.** Both parties will comply with the Privacy Act 2020 in respect of any personal data handled under this Agreement.

### 4. Intellectual Property

4.1 Sport Waikato retains all intellectual property in the REAP brand, platform, and product. This Agreement grants Sponsor a limited, non-exclusive licence to use the REAP name and logo solely in association with the specific sponsorship described in Clause 1, during the season period.

4.2 Sponsor retains all IP in its own brand assets. Sport Waikato's use of Sponsor's logo is solely for the purposes described in Clause 1.

4.3 Neither party may use the other's brand assets outside the scope of this Agreement without prior written consent.

---

### 5. Competitive Exclusivity

5.1 **Category exclusivity.** Sport Waikato grants Sponsor exclusive category sponsorship for the duration of the Season. Sport Waikato will not accept sponsorship from a direct competitor of Sponsor (defined as [category definition — e.g., "a company whose primary business is wearable fitness devices"]) during the Season covered by this Agreement.

5.2 **Competitor participation.** Nothing in this Agreement prevents Sport Waikato from accepting participants who are employees or customers of Sponsor's competitors. Competitive exclusivity applies to sponsorship only.

5.3 **Competitor threat notice.** If a direct competitor of Sponsor (as defined in 5.1) launches a product that materially replicates REAP's model during the term of this Agreement, Sport Waikato will notify Sponsor within 14 days. Sport Waikato's response to competitive threats is set out in its Competitive Strategy (internal document). Sponsor's rights are not affected by the emergence of a competitor — this Agreement remains in force for its full term regardless.

5.4 **Right of first refusal.** Sponsor has a right of first refusal to renew its sponsorship category for the following season at equivalent terms. Sport Waikato will contact Sponsor at least 60 days before the following season commences to confirm whether Sponsor wishes to exercise this right.

---

### 6. Reputation and Values

6.1 Sport Waikato reserves the right to terminate this Agreement immediately if Sponsor:
- Is found to have engaged in conduct that is materially inconsistent with Sport Waikato's values or charitable mandate
- Makes public statements that misrepresent REAP, its prize structure, or its legal compliance status
- Is the subject of regulatory action, serious public controversy, or reputational events that Sport Waikato reasonably determines would damage REAP's reputation if the association continued

6.2 On termination under 6.1, Sport Waikato will refund a pro-rata portion of the Sponsorship Fee for the unused portion of the Season.

6.3 Sponsor may terminate this Agreement if Sport Waikato:
- Is found to have operated REAP in violation of the Gambling Act 2003 or other material regulatory requirement
- Experiences a serious data breach affecting participant data
- Fails to deliver the sponsorship benefits described in Clause 1 after 14 days' written notice
