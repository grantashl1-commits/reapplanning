## PART ONE: RECOMMENDED NZ AMBASSADOR PROFILES

Before the agreement template, the following is a shortlist of New Zealand personalities who represent a strong fit for REAP's October 2026 soft launch. These are profile recommendations, not confirmed engagements.

---

### Tier 1 — Highest Impact Recommendations

**Portia Woodman-Wickliffe**
*Black Ferns, Rugby World Cup champion*
Rationale: Elite athlete, women's health role model, massive post-World Cup profile, authentically credible in a daily movement product. Would bring serious fitness credibility to REAP and connect with the 25–45 women's demographic. Her social media following is engaged and health-focused. Potential Waikato connections through NZRU. If she survives the month — extraordinary. If she gets eliminated — equally compelling content.
Risk profile: Low. Clean public profile, aligned values, genuine sport mandate.

**Nigel Latta**
*Psychologist, TV presenter (TVNZ)*
Rationale: Would give REAP intellectual credibility beyond the "fun game" framing. Can explain the behavioural science behind loss aversion and commitment devices in plain language — which is exactly what media will ask about. Respected broadly across demographics, particularly by people who might otherwise dismiss the concept. His endorsement signals that the science is real.
Risk profile: Low. Excellent public reputation, credible, articulate.

**Sam Wallace**
*TVNZ Sport presenter*
Rationale: Large, loyal TV audience. Credible in sport and health contexts. Likely to engage genuinely with the challenge and produce authentic content. Would bring sports community connections and mainstream media reach.
Risk profile: Low.

### Tier 3 — Specialty Consideration

**A NZ GP or sports medicine physician (unnamed — introduce via NZMA)**
Rationale: One medical professional in the soft launch cohort gives REAP clinical credibility and directly answers the "is this healthy?" question. Doesn't need to be a celebrity — needs to be credible and willing to speak to the health rationale on the record.

**A NZ mental health ambassador (e.g., Mental Health Foundation spokesperson)**
Rationale: Directly addresses the most significant reputational risk. A mental health professional participating in REAP and speaking positively about its design for wellbeing is far more powerful than any disclaimer or Terms language. Explore this in parallel with the formal mental health partnership discussion.

---

### What to Avoid

- Celebrities with active gambling-adjacent controversies
- Sports personalities currently under investigation or in contract disputes
- Any personality whose audience is primarily under 18
- Micro-influencers with follower engagement that is artificially inflated
- Celebrities who are known to have significant dietary restriction or eating disorder histories (elimination mechanic + compulsive exercise risk)

### 1. Background

Sport Waikato operates REAP — Survive the Reap — a 30-day daily movement survival game launching publicly on 1 November 2026. The Ambassador has been invited to participate in the October 2026 celebrity soft launch season (the "Season") as part of REAP's pre-launch awareness campaign.

The Ambassador's participation is gifted — no subscription fee is charged. The Ambassador's role is to participate genuinely in the Season and share their experience authentically with their audience.

---

### 2. Ambassador Obligations

**2.1 Genuine participation.** The Ambassador agrees to make a genuine effort to complete 21 minutes of Zone 2 cardiovascular movement each day during the Season. The Ambassador acknowledges that elimination is a designed game outcome and that being eliminated (including on Day 1) does not constitute a failure of this Agreement.

**2.2 Minimum content.** The Ambassador agrees to create and publish a minimum of **four pieces of content** during October 2026 on their primary social media platform(s), comprising:

- **Post 1:** Season start (1 October or within the first 3 days) — announcing participation
- **Post 2:** Mid-season check-in (between Days 10–20) — update on survival status
- **Post 3:** Elimination or survival — authentic response at the point of elimination or at season end
- **Post 4:** Call to action — encouraging their audience to sign up for public Season 1 (November 2026)

Additional content is welcome and encouraged. Content may be posts, stories, reels, videos, or any format suited to the Ambassador's platform.

**2.3 Authenticity.** Content must be the Ambassador's genuine voice and experience. Sport Waikato will not script, pre-approve (unless asked), or require specific language. Authentic accounts of struggling with the daily requirement, or being eliminated early, are entirely acceptable and are part of the product's intended narrative.

**2.4 Disclosure.** All content must include an appropriate disclosure that the Ambassador's participation is gifted (e.g., #gifted, #ad, or similar in accordance with the Ambassador's local advertising standards obligations).

**2.5 App access and data.** The Ambassador agrees to register on the REAP platform using their real date of birth (age verification), connect a compatible activity tracking device or app, and allow the REAP platform to access their daily activity data during the Season. Activity data will be handled in accordance with REAP's Privacy Policy.

---

### 3. Restrictions

The Ambassador agrees not to:

**3.1** Describe REAP, its prize structure, or its subscription model as gambling, a lottery, a prize competition, or as a product that offers a chance to win money in exchange for payment. This language is legally inaccurate and is specifically prohibited.

**3.2** Disclose any prize amounts, prize values, or prize eligibility criteria without prior written approval from Sport Waikato.

**3.3** Make any false, misleading, or unsubstantiated claims about REAP's health benefits (e.g., specific weight loss or disease prevention claims).

**3.4** Disclose any confidential product information provided by Sport Waikato before the public launch date of 1 November 2026 (see Clause 7).

**3.5** Encourage their audience to use the product in a manner inconsistent with the Season Rules or Terms of Participation.

---

### 4. Sport Waikato's Obligations

**4.1 Gifted season pass.** Sport Waikato will provide the Ambassador with a gifted October 2026 season entry at no charge.

**4.2 Product information.** Sport Waikato will provide the Ambassador with a clear briefing document covering how the product works, the daily requirement, Redemption Days, and prize eligibility before the Season starts.

**4.3 Support.** A dedicated contact at Sport Waikato will be available to the Ambassador throughout October for any questions, technical issues, or disputes.

**4.4 Content resharing.** Sport Waikato may reshare the Ambassador's public content (with appropriate credit) on REAP's own social media channels and website. Sport Waikato will not edit the Ambassador's content before resharing.

**4.5 Future partnership.** If the soft launch partnership is successful, Sport Waikato may approach the Ambassador about a paid ambassador arrangement for Season 1 or subsequent seasons. This Agreement does not constitute a commitment to such an arrangement by either party.

---

### 5. Intellectual Property

**5.1** Content created by the Ambassador remains the property of the Ambassador.

**5.2** Sport Waikato grants the Ambassador a limited licence to use the REAP name, logo, and brand assets in content created under this Agreement, in accordance with any brand guidelines provided.

**5.3** The Ambassador grants Sport Waikato a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to reshare content created under this Agreement on Sport Waikato's owned channels, for the duration of the Season and for marketing purposes for a period of 12 months thereafter.

---

### 6. Controversy and Termination

**6.1 Ambassador conduct.** Sport Waikato may terminate this Agreement immediately if the Ambassador:
- Is the subject of credible public allegations of serious misconduct (violence, fraud, dishonesty, or conduct materially inconsistent with Sport Waikato's values)
- Makes public statements that are materially negative about REAP's primary purpose (daily movement and health), denigrating the product's value to participants beyond honest personal experience
- Breaches any restriction in Clause 3

**6.2 Termination effect.** On termination, Sport Waikato may remove the Ambassador's content from its own channels and cease association. The Ambassador may retain content they have published independently. No compensation is payable on termination for Ambassador conduct.

**6.3 Ambassador's right to exit.** The Ambassador may withdraw from the Agreement at any time with 48 hours' notice. In this case, they are asked (but not required) to publish one final post acknowledging withdrawal and Season 2 registration. No penalty applies.

**6.4 Product discontinuation.** If Sport Waikato discontinues REAP before the Season ends, this Agreement terminates automatically and the Ambassador has no further obligations.

---

### 7. Confidentiality

The Ambassador acknowledges that product details, launch plans, legal documents, financial projections, and any other information marked as confidential and shared in the briefing process are confidential to Sport Waikato. This obligation survives termination and lasts for 12 months from the date of this Agreement.

**8.1** The Ambassador participates in REAP at their own physical risk. Sport Waikato is not liable for any physical injury or health consequence of the Ambassador's participation in the daily movement requirement.

**8.2** The Ambassador acknowledges having read the REAP Terms of Participation and Season Rules and accepts their terms.

**8.3** The Ambassador is responsible for their own content, including for compliance with applicable advertising standards and social media platform policies.

**9.2 Entire agreement:** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding the soft launch participation and supersedes any prior discussion or correspondence.

## PART THREE: AMBASSADOR BRIEFING DOCUMENT

*To be provided to each Ambassador alongside the signed Agreement.*

**What REAP is:**
REAP is a 30-day daily movement survival game. Every day you must complete 21 minutes of Zone 2 cardiovascular movement — that's moderate aerobic effort, achievable by walking briskly, cycling, swimming, or light running. Log it via your connected device (or manually if needed) before midnight. At 23:59 NZST each night, anyone who hasn't hit 21 minutes is eliminated. Permanently, for that season.

**What Zone 2 means:**
The intensity where you can hold a conversation but it takes a bit of effort. 60–70% of max heart rate. Think: brisk walk, easy jog, steady bike. Not a sprint.

**Your Redemption Days:**
You have two Redemption Days per season. These must be declared in advance (in the app) on a day you know you won't be able to move. Use them wisely.

**What the prize is:**
There are performance prizes at milestone points during the season — awarded to the participants who have most demonstrably moved during each measurement period. You are eligible for these if you're still alive. You do not need to promote prizes in your content. Just talk about the game.

**What we'd love from your content:**
Be honest. Be yourself. If you find it hard, say so. If you get eliminated, film your reaction. The best content from this launch will be authentic, not polished. The game is better when people see how real it is.

**What not to say:**
- Don't describe the subscription as a "prize entry fee" or "gambling" or "lottery"
- Don't quote specific prize dollar amounts in public posts
- Don't make health claims about weight loss or disease prevention
- Don't mention anything in our legal or financial documents that was shared in confidence
