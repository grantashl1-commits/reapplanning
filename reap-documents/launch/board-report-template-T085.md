## Document Purpose

This document provides the complete template and guidance for the REAP Season 1 mid-season board report. The mid-season report is a formal board governance document. It presents KPI performance against targets, financial results, welfare incidents, legal and compliance status, and requests any board decisions required to proceed with Season 2.

**Reporting period:** Season 1 (1–30 November 2026) — mid-season snapshot at Day 15 (15 November 2026), full report prepared 1 December 2026 using final Season 1 data.

**Note on timing:** Given Season 1 runs the full month of November, the "mid-season" board report is delivered at Season 1 close (1 December 2026) rather than at an interim point. It serves as both a mid-season update and an end-of-Season-1 report for board governance purposes. If the board meets before Season 1 closes, Leanne delivers an oral update only; the written report follows at season close.

---

## Presentation Format Recommendation

**Recommendation: Written report with a 5-slide executive summary deck.**

**Rationale:**
- A full written report provides the audit trail and specificity that board governance requires. Trustees can review at their own pace and ask informed questions.
- A 5-slide deck (prepared from the written report) ensures the board meeting stays focused and efficient.
- The written report is filed as a board paper. The slides are the meeting tool.

**Slide structure (5 slides only):**
1. Season 1 in numbers (KPI dashboard — traffic-light table)
2. Financial performance (revenue vs. model)
3. Welfare and compliance (incident summary, DIA status)
4. What we're changing for Season 2
5. Decisions required from the board today

**Distribution:** Board receives the full written report 5 calendar days before the board meeting. The slide deck is presented at the meeting by Leanne.
