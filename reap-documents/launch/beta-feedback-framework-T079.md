## 1. Overview

This document defines how Sport Waikato collects, analyses, and acts on feedback from the 50 beta participants in the October 2026 Survive the Reap soft launch. The purpose of structured beta feedback is not validation — it is discovery. We are specifically hunting for evidence of anything that could embarrass, harm, or break the product when exposed to 500+ paying subscribers in November 2026.

**Feedback closes:** 12 October 2026  
**Analysis:** 13–14 October 2026  
**Report to Leanne:** 15 October 2026  
**Go/No-Go decision:** 25 October 2026

## 2. Feedback Collection Methods

### 2.1 Method Summary

| Method | Audience | When | Response Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| **In-app feedback prompt (Day 7)** | All 50 beta participants | 7 October (mid-beta) | 80% response rate |
| **Closing email survey (full)** | All 50 beta participants | 14 October (beta close) | 80% response rate |
| **1:1 interviews (30 minutes)** | 10 selected participants | 13–14 October | 100% of scheduled |
| **In-app bug/issue reports (ongoing)** | All 50 beta participants | Throughout beta | All captured; logged in Issues Log |
| **Daily async Slack/email channel** | All 50 beta participants | Throughout beta | Captured daily by Ashleigh |

### 2.2 In-App Day 7 Prompt

A short 3-question in-app pulse check appears on Day 7 (7 October) for all active participants.

**Day 7 questions:**
1. How easy has it been to connect and sync your device? (1–5 stars)
2. Do you clearly understand how to earn your daily Zone 2 credit? (Yes / Mostly / No — [text box if No])
3. What's the one thing you'd change about REAP right now? (open text)

This is not the full survey — it is an early signal for Ashleigh to identify systemic onboarding failures while there is still time to communicate a fix during the beta.

### 2.3 Closing Email Survey (Full)

Sent on 14 October 2026 at 9:00am NZT via Resend. Survey is hosted in Typeform or equivalent. Link is unique per participant (prevents duplicate submission). Survey closes 12:00 noon, 12 October — wait, the closing email is sent on Day 14 (14 Oct); survey closes EOD 12 October for anyone who has already been eliminated/exited, or 14 October night for those still active.

**Reminder sent:** 15 October (morning) to non-respondents.

## 5. Beta Metrics Extracted from Database

At beta close (14 October), Ashleigh runs the following queries against the Supabase production database and pulls reports from TERRA and Resend.

| Metric | Data Source | Query Method |
|---|---|---|
| Daily active users (DAU) by day | Supabase `activity_logs` | `COUNT DISTINCT participant_id WHERE activity_date = [date]` |

## 6. Analysis Framework

### 6.1 Issue Severity Classification

Every piece of feedback — survey response, bug report, or interview note — is classified by two dimensions: **severity** and **frequency**.

| Severity | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Critical** | Safety risk, incorrect elimination, data exposure, payment failure, welfare inadequacy |
| **High** | Core game loop broken or confusing for > 20% of participants |
| **Medium** | Friction in non-core flows (onboarding, settings) affecting experience but not game integrity |
| **Low** | Cosmetic, minor UX polish, nice-to-have improvements |

| Frequency | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Systemic** | Reported by ≥ 30% of participants (15+ of 50) |
| **Common** | Reported by 10–29% of participants (5–14 of 50) |
| **Isolated** | Reported by < 10% of participants (< 5 of 50) |

**Priority matrix:**

| | Critical | High | Medium | Low |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Systemic** | Fix immediately (launch blocker) | Fix before launch (P1) | Fix before launch (P1) | Fix in Season 1 (P2) |
| **Common** | Fix immediately (launch blocker) | Fix before launch (P1) | Fix in Season 1 (P2) | Backlog (P3) |
| **Isolated** | Fix immediately (launch blocker) | Fix before launch (P1) | Backlog (P3) | Backlog (P3) |

### 6.2 Frequency Analysis

For each quantitative question in the survey, Ashleigh produces:
- Mean score and distribution chart
- Percentage of participants scoring ≤ 3 on any 5-point scale (indicates dissatisfaction)
- Percentage of each multiple-choice option selected
- Thematic grouping of open-text responses (use tags: DEVICE, ONBOARDING, ZONE2, ELIMINATION, WELFARE, PRICE, GAME_MECHANIC, APP_BUG)

### 6.3 Qualitative Thematic Coding

All open-text responses are read in full and tagged with one or more of the following themes:

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| DEVICE | Device connectivity or sync issues |
| ONBOARDING | Registration, setup, or first-day friction |
| ZONE2 | Confusion or issues with Zone 2 understanding or detection |
| ELIMINATION | Elimination notification experience (clarity, emotion, fairness) |
| WELFARE | Feelings of pressure, stress, or inadequate support |
| PRICE | Perception of $13/month value |
| GAME_MECHANIC | Response to survival game format, Redemption Day, etc. |
| APP_BUG | Technical bugs reported |
| POSITIVE | Positive feedback to preserve |
| CONFUSION | General confusion not captured in above tags |

## 8. Decision Tree: Beta Findings → Launch Decision

```
Start: Beta Report Complete (15 October 2026)
│
├─ Are there ANY open P0 bugs?
│   ├─ YES → DELAY or FIX (Dev Team must resolve all P0s before launch. Re-test. New Go/No-Go date.)
│   └─ NO → Continue
│
├─ Is elimination accuracy 100%?
│   ├─ NO → DELAY (Elimination logic is core product integrity. No exceptions.)
│   └─ YES → Continue
│
├─ Is device sync success rate ≥ 90%?
│   ├─ NO → PROCEED WITH FIXES (Identify failing device types; implement fix or communicate workaround; re-test in staging)
│   └─ YES → Continue
│
├─ Were there any Tier 2 welfare incidents attributable to app behaviour?
│   ├─ YES → PAUSE (Review with Leanne and medical advisor; determine if protocol change required before launch)
│   └─ NO → Continue
│
├─ Is participant NPS ≥ 30?
│   ├─ NO → REVIEW (NPS below 30 = insufficient enthusiasm; review root cause; may proceed if high-severity issues resolved)
│   └─ YES → Continue
│
├─ Are all Critical/Systemic issues resolved or have an accepted mitigation plan?
│   ├─ NO → PROCEED WITH FIXES (Must have a written fix plan with completion by 20 Oct for each)
│   └─ YES → Continue
│
└─ PROCEED TO LAUNCH (1 November 2026)
```

## 9. Critical Issue Threshold: What Qualifies as a Launch-Blocking Issue

A launch-blocking issue (P0) is any bug or finding that meets any one of the following criteria:

| Criterion | Example |
|---|---|
| Causes or risks incorrect elimination | A participant meets 21+ minutes but is eliminated; or a participant does not meet requirement but is not eliminated |
| Exposes participant health or personal data to other participants or the public | TERRA HR data visible to wrong user; Supabase RLS failure |
| Allows payment bypass or failed Stripe charging | Participant accesses full Season 1 without being charged |
| Prevents Zone 2 activity from being recorded | TERRA webhook silently failing; session not written to DB |
| Breaks welfare notification delivery | Elimination email not sent; welfare check message not delivered |
| Causes data loss (activity logs deleted or overwritten) | Edge case in cron job overwrites prior day's logs |
| Any Tier 2 welfare incident attributable to a preventable system failure | Participant not receiving welfare check message due to Resend configuration error |

**A finding is also launch-blocking if:**
- More than 15 of 50 beta participants (30%+) are unable to complete a valid Zone 2 session due to a systemic issue (device, app, or network), and no workaround exists.
- The medical advisor raises a protocol concern that has not been resolved.
