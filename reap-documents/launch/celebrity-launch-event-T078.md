## 1. Event Concept and Purpose

### 1.1 Concept

The REAP Celebrity Soft Launch Event is a curated, media-ready activation designed to generate earned media coverage, social content, and public awareness of Survive the Reap ahead of the 1 November 2026 Season 1 public launch. The event is anchored by Sport Waikato's celebrity ambassador and tells a simple story: **a bold new fitness survival game built in Hamilton, for New Zealand.**

This is not a product demo. It is a story launch. Every element — venue, visual, activity, statement — is designed to produce a shareable moment that media and audiences will amplify.

### 1.2 Purpose

| Purpose | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Generate earned media | At least 3 Hamilton/Waikato media placements (Waikato Times, RNZ, community outlets) |
| Launch social content | Anchor content for Instagram, TikTok, Facebook that seeds organic reach |
| Establish ambassador credibility | Cement celebrity ambassador as the face and voice of REAP |
| Signal to funders and stakeholders | Demonstrate Sport Waikato's ability to execute at a professional standard |
| Drive pre-launch sign-ups | Create urgency and curiosity to drive Season 1 subscription intent |

### 1.3 Key Messages for the Day

All communications, ambassador talking points, and media Q&A pivot back to these three messages:

1. **"REAP is New Zealand's first fitness survival game."** — First mover, NZ innovation, born in Waikato.
2. **"21 minutes every day. That's all it takes — but it has to be real."** — Simplicity of the challenge; no gimmicks.
3. **"Sport Waikato is using REAP to get more Waikato people moving."** — Community health mission; not just entertainment.

## 2. Event Format Options

### 2.1 Option A — Press Breakfast (Indoor)
An intimate breakfast at a Hamilton venue. Media, influencers, and stakeholders attend. Ambassador presents; media Q&A; product demo stations set up around the room. Social content captured throughout.

**Pros:** Controlled environment, comfortable for media, high production quality possible.  
**Cons:** Less visual dynamism; harder to produce standout social content in a seated breakfast format.

### 2.2 Option B — Outdoor Activation (Community Park / Public Space)
Ambassador leads a Zone 2 group walk or jog in a Hamilton park (e.g., Hamilton Lake Domain). Media and invited guests participate. Live wearable tracking displayed on a screen or tablet. Photo opportunity: ambassador in motion, participants moving together, REAP branding backdrop.

**Pros:** Highly visual; ambassador looks authentic doing the actual challenge; embodies the product; great for photo and video content.  
**Cons:** Weather-dependent; harder to control media access; acoustic challenges for Q&A.

### 2.3 Option C — Social Media Livestream (Digital First)
Ambassador hosts a 30-minute Instagram/Facebook live event. Tours the REAP app, does a Zone 2 walk live on camera, takes questions from viewers. Promoted with media invite to watch and report.

**Pros:** Zero venue cost; maximum reach if ambassador has significant following; easy to produce.  
**Cons:** No in-person moment; limited photo opportunities; less prestigious for stakeholders and funders; harder to generate traditional media coverage.

### 2.4 Option D — Hybrid (Recommended)
An outdoor morning activation (Zone 2 group walk/jog, 21 minutes) at Hamilton Lake Domain, followed by a short media breakfast at an indoor Hamilton venue. The outdoor section produces visual content; the indoor section provides controlled media Q&A and stakeholder time.

---

## 3. Recommended Format: Hybrid (Option D)

**Rationale:**

The hybrid format is recommended because:
- **Authenticity**: The ambassador and guests completing a real Zone 2 session demonstrates the product, not just talks about it. The best content is people actually doing the thing.
- **Visual variety**: Outdoor motion shots and candid moments, plus clean indoor press conference imagery — content suitable for multiple platforms and formats.
- **Media comfort**: Journalists get a great photo opportunity outside and a comfortable Q&A environment inside. Both needs are met.
- **Weather resilience**: The outdoor section is 21 minutes only, minimising weather risk. Indoor venue is booked regardless.
- **Funder/board impression**: The indoor breakfast gives Sport Waikato board members and funders a dignified moment with the ambassador and media.

**Recommended outdoor venue:** Hamilton Lake Domain (Te Roto o Waikato), west shoreline path. Flat, visually attractive, well-known Hamilton landmark, accessible, parking available.  
**Recommended indoor venue:** Waikato Museum (Te Whare Taonga o Waikato) — see Section 5.

## 7. Ambassador Briefing

### 7.1 What the Ambassador Is Asked to Do on the Day

1. **Lead the Zone 2 walk/jog** at Hamilton Lake Domain — 21 minutes, maintaining conversational pace with guests, narrating what Zone 2 feels like as they go.
2. **Deliver an 8-minute address** at the Waikato Museum — personal story, REAP endorsement, Season 1 call to action.
3. **Participate in media Q&A** — 15 minutes alongside Leanne. Comms Lead will prep likely questions.
4. **Participate in influencer content moments** — short to-camera content with creators.
5. **Sign off on social posts** before they are published under their handle.

### 7.2 Ambassador Talking Points

| Topic | Talking Point |
|---|---|
| Why REAP | "I've been involved in sport my whole career, and the thing that changes lives isn't elite performance — it's daily movement. REAP makes that feel real. There are stakes. It's a game. And 21 minutes is achievable for almost anyone." |
| Zone 2 | "Zone 2 is the kind of effort you can sustain. We're not asking for brutal — we're asking for consistent. That's the habit that changes your health." |
| Waikato | "I love that this was built here in Hamilton. Sport Waikato has been doing incredible work for years, and REAP feels like a genuine next step." |
| Season 1 | "Season 1 starts November 1. Sign up. Do the 21 minutes. Stay alive." |

### 7.3 Media Questions to Prepare

| Likely Question | Suggested Response Direction |
|---|---|
| "Is this gambling?" | "No — it's a subscription challenge, not gambling. You pay for the experience and the motivation. The prize is incidental." |
| "Is 21 minutes every day achievable?" | "Absolutely. That's a brisk walk. We're not asking for a gym session." |
| "What if you get sick?" | "There's a medical hold — you can pause without losing your place. REAP is designed to be fair." |
| "Why Hamilton?" | "Sport Waikato is a Waikato organisation. They built this for their community first." |

### 7.4 Ambassador Do's and Don'ts on the Day

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Speak from personal experience | Don't make specific medical claims |
| Reference REAP as a "challenge" and "game" | Don't reference it as gambling or a lottery |
| Use first name basis with media | Don't reveal beta participant names or data |
| Engage warmly with every guest | Don't discuss fees/revenue specifics |

## 11. Contingency Plans

### 11.1 Adverse Weather (Outdoor Activation Cancelled)

**Trigger:** Rain exceeding light drizzle, wind >30km/h, or temperature <8°C at 6:00am on event day.

**Response:**
- The outdoor Zone 2 walk is cancelled. Guests proceed directly to the indoor venue (Waikato Museum).
- Ambassador does a 10-minute indoor treadmill or stationary bike demonstration inside the museum (pre-arranged with venue to have one treadmill set up in the event space as a contingency prop).
- Content Lead captures ambassador on treadmill with REAP app displayed — produces equivalent hero content.
- Run-of-show compressed: ambassador address extended to include Zone 2 explanation that would have been demonstrated outdoors.
- All outdoor timing blocks shift 30 minutes forward; total run-of-show shortened by 30 minutes.

**Decision point:** Weather decision made by Leanne at 5:30am on event day. Content Lead and Production crew notified by 5:45am.

### 11.2 Ambassador Cancellation

**Trigger:** Ambassador cancels or becomes unavailable within 48 hours of event.

**Response:**
- If cancelled > 48 hours before event: reschedule ambassador for a separate content capture day (video only). Event proceeds without ambassador as a "Sport Waikato product announcement" event. Leanne leads all media-facing moments.
- If cancelled < 48 hours: same as above. Leanne leads; event format remains intact (outdoor walk proceeds; indoor breakfast proceeds). Media are notified of format change by Comms Lead.
- Pre-filmed ambassador content (if available) may be played on screen during the breakfast.
- Post-event, ambassador records a video message for distribution as a social content post within 7 days.

### 11.3 Media No-Shows

**Trigger:** Fewer than 2 media outlets attend the event.

**Response:**
- Event proceeds as planned regardless.
- Comms Lead follows up all no-show media contacts with press kit and exclusive ambassador quote offer (arranged phone/Zoom interview with ambassador).
- Sport Waikato's owned channels become the primary distribution mechanism.
- Paid social promotion considered (small budget boost on ambassador content from Sport Waikato's social accounts).

### 11.4 Technical Failure (App Crashes During Live Demo)

**Trigger:** App crashes or fails to load during the indoor product demonstration.

**Response:**
- Ashleigh immediately switches to screen-recorded demo video (pre-recorded and loaded on a second device as a backup).
- Comms Lead fills with a brief "technology being technology" moment — audience response is typically good-humoured.
- Demo video is played; Ashleigh narrates live.
