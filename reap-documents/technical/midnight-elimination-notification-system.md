# Midnight Elimination Notification System

**Prepared by:** Dev Team
**Date:** 2026-04-09
**Status:** Draft

## Purpose
Document the notification workflow for elimination events triggered at midnight NZT.

## Requirements
- Detect elimination events after daily Zone 2 movement validation
- Deliver push notifications to affected participants immediately after midnight NZT
- Include welfare check links and support contact information
- Log notification delivery status for audit and escalation

## Acceptance criteria
- Notification workflow defined for elimination event generation
- Delivery channels and retry strategy specified
- Welfare escalation logic documented for high-risk participants
