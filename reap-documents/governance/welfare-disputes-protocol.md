# REAP Welfare and Disputes Protocol
**Sport Waikato Incorporated | Governance Protocol**

## 1. Purpose
This protocol describes how REAP manages participant welfare concerns, disputes, and appeals in a safe, fair, and transparent way.

## 2. Principles
- Participant wellbeing is the highest priority.
- Disputes are handled promptly and with respect.
- Decisions are evidence-based and documented.
- A welfare-first approach is applied before competitive outcomes.

## 3. Welfare Escalation
1. Participants may raise welfare concerns through the REAP support channel or email.
2. Welfare concerns are triaged by the welfare officer within one business day.
3. If a participant is assessed as being at risk of harm, the welfare officer may suspend their season participation and offer support resources.
4. Any decision to temporarily remove a participant from a season for welfare reasons is reviewed by a designated senior lead.

## 4. Dispute Process
1. Participants may dispute elimination decisions within 48 hours of being eliminated.
2. Disputes must include the participant's name, account details, season and day of the issue, and a description of the problem.
3. The dispute is reviewed by the operations team and welfare officer.
4. If the dispute involves a documented system error, the participant may be reinstated at Sport Waikato's discretion.

## 5. Appeal Process
1. If a participant is dissatisfied with the dispute outcome, they may appeal to a senior governance representative.
2. Appeals are assessed based on documented facts, participant communications, and the original decision rationale.
3. Appeals are final unless new evidence emerges that materially changes the assessment.

## 6. Record Keeping
- All welfare cases, disputes, and appeals are logged in a secure case management system.
- Records include dates, correspondence, evidence reviewed, and final decisions.
- Confidential participant information is protected in line with the Privacy Policy.

## 7. Communication
- Participants receive clear confirmation when their dispute is received.
- Outcome communications explain the reason for the decision and any next steps.
- Welfare-sensitive communications use non-stigmatising language and offer support options.

## 8. Review
This protocol is reviewed at least annually and whenever a material welfare or dispute issue arises.
