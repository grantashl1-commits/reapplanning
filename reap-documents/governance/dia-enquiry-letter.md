## 3. Competition & Gambling Law Compliance Controls

REAP operates seven spot prize draws per season (NZD $666 per draw; total $4,662 per season). We confirm the following controls:

**Prize Structure Classification:**
The REAP prize draws are structured as **promotional competitions**, not gambling, based on the following framework:

- **Consideration:** Participants pay NZD $13.00 per month for access to the REAP platform and game mechanics — not for entry into the prize draw
- **Automatic eligibility:** Active, confirmed-paid survivors are automatically eligible for draws; no separate payment or entry process is required
- **Random selection:** Winners are selected using a certified random process (method: [SPECIFY: e.g., cryptographic randomisation])
- **Prize fund source:** Entirely from Sport Waikato operational budget, segregated from subscription revenue at accounting level
- **Frequency and scale:** Seven draws per season, each NZD $666, are proportionate to prize-pool infrastructure

**Legal Classification:**

Under the **Gambling Act 2003** and supporting case law, this model qualifies as a lawful promotional competition because:
1. Entry cannot be obtained solely by payment (payment is for the game, not eligibility)
2. Winners are determined by chance applied to an eligible pool
3. Consideration paid by participants is clearly for a distinct service (game access)
4. Prize fund is transparent and documented

## 4. Participant Consent Process

**Data Collection Consent:**

During registration, participants are presented with a layered consent model:

- **Tier 1 (Required):** Consent to Terms and Conditions, Privacy Policy, and Refund Policy. This is mandatory to proceed.
- **Tier 2 (Optional):** Consent to research participation and anonymised data sharing. Participants may opt in or decline.
- **Tier 3 (Optional):** Consent to communications from sponsoring partners. Participants may opt in or decline.

**Evidence of Consent:**

- Digital timestamp of acceptance during registration flow
- Checkbox confirmation for each tier
- Email receipt sent to participant with record of selections
- All consent data stored in compliance with Privacy Act 2020

**Research Participation:**

Participants who opt into research receive:
- Clear explanation of what data will be collected and analysed
- Assurance that data will be anonymised and aggregated
- Invitation to view research findings once published
- Annual opt-out option (updated at season start)

**Supporting Documentation:**
