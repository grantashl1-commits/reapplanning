# REAP Equity and Access Policy
**Sport Waikato Incorporated | Governance Policy**

## 1. Policy Statement
Sport Waikato is committed to equitable access to REAP participation. Our goal is to ensure the game is accessible, safe, and reasonably affordable for communities with diverse needs.

## 2. Accessibility Principles
- REAP should be available to participants of all genders, ethnicities, and socioeconomic backgrounds.
- Communication and user journeys should use clear, inclusive language.
- We will provide reasonable accommodation for participants with disabilities wherever operationally feasible.
- We will monitor participation patterns to identify and address any barriers to access.

## 3. Cost and Affordability
- The standard subscription fee is NZD $13.00 per season.
- Participants experiencing financial hardship may be offered a hardship support pathway, which could include free or discounted access subject to board-approved criteria.
- Hardship support is discretionary and assessed case by case.

## 4. Welfare and Health Equity
- We recognise that some participants may have complex health needs.
- REAP is not a clinical service. Participants with medical concerns should seek professional advice before joining.
- The welfare policy includes clear non-stigmatising guidance for participants who need to step away from the game for safety reasons.

## 5. Digital Inclusion
- REAP will provide device-agnostic participation options, including manual submission for participants whose wearable device fails or who do not own a compatible tracker.
- We will maintain accessible communications channels for support, including email and app-based help.

## 6. Reporting and Review
- Sport Waikato will review this policy annually and whenever new accessibility or equity issues are identified.
- Governance will track metrics on demographic representation, hardship support requests, and access barriers.
- The board will be informed of any material changes to access or equity practice.
