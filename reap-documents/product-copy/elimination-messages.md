# REAP Elimination Messages
**Sport Waikato Incorporated | Product Copy**

## Standard Elimination Message
Subject: REAP Update — You have been eliminated

Hi [Name],

Today we checked your activity for Day [#]. Unfortunately we did not receive a valid 21-minute Zone 2 activity submission from you before the 23:45 NZST cutoff.

This means you have been eliminated from the current REAP season.

What happens now:
- Your account remains active for future seasons.
- You can still review your progress in the app.
- You may sign up again for the next season.

Please remember that this is a designed part of the game. We encourage you to keep moving and come back stronger.

## Redemption Day Elimination Message
Subject: REAP Update — Redemption Day used

Hi [Name],

You used a Redemption Day today, so you remain alive in the season even though you did not complete your normal activity requirement.

Keep going — you still have the remainder of the season ahead of you.
