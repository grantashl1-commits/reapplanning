### Email 4: Redemption Days Explained
**Subject:** Your Free Passes — How to Use Redemption Days  
**Sent:** Day 2  
**Purpose:** Clarify mechanics, reduce anxiety

---

Hi [Name],

You have **two Redemption Days** this season. Here's what they do — and how to use them wisely.

**What's a Redemption Day?**

A Redemption Day protects you from elimination for one day. Even if you don't do your 21 minutes, you stay alive.

It's a safety net. Not a cheat code.

**When to use one:**
- You're sick or injured
- Life happens — unexpected emergency, family crisis, work overload
- You need a genuine rest day
- You're mentally burned out and need a pause

**When to NOT use one:**
- You forgot to track
- You're bored (just because it's hard on Day 2 doesn't mean you quit)
- You're saving them "just in case" — that's fear talking, not strategy

**How to declare one:**

Go to the app → Season Settings → Redemption Days, and click "Use Today." You can do this anytime before 23:59 NZST.

**The strategy:**

Some people save both for later. Some use one early. There's no perfect move — just pick what feels right for you.

Keep going,  
The REAP Team

## Sending Guidelines

### Frequency
- **Pre-season:** 4 emails over 7 days leading up to launch
- **Active season:** 1 reminder per day (optional, customizable) + periodic check-ins
- **Post-elimination:** 2 emails (immediate + day after)
- **Winner:** 3 emails (notification + payment + feature)

### Best Practices
1. **Timing:** Send prime-time reminders mid-afternoon (16:00–18:00 NZST) to catch people thinking about their evening activity
2. **Tone:** Warm, conversational, non-judgmental — like a friend, not a corporation
3. **Mobile-first:** All templates are optimized for mobile reading
4. **Unsubscribe:** Always include an easy way to adjust email frequency or opt out
5. **A/B Testing:** Monitor open rates and adjust subject lines based on performance
