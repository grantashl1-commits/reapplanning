# Influencer / Micro-Influencer Outreach List

**Prepared by:** Marketing Team
**Date:** 2026-04-09
**Status:** Draft

## Purpose
Create a practical outreach list for local NZ influencers and micro-influencers who can authentically amplify REAP as a Hamilton-led wellbeing challenge, not a retro gimmick.

## Strategy
- Target creators with strong local credibility in Waikato, Hamilton, or adjacent regions.
- Position REAP as a bold, evidence-based 30-day movement survival game from Sport Waikato with a strong community mission.
- Prioritise authenticity: creators should be able to share real progress, real struggle, and the emotional lift of surviving the challenge.

## Primary outreach audience
- Waikato and Hamilton wellness coaches, movement specialists, and physiotherapists with active local followings.
- Local lifestyle creators who speak to busy adults, parents, and people trying to fit better habits into everyday life.
- Health, wellbeing and corporate wellness micro-influencers with engaged NZ audiences.
- Community sports voices, walking/running groups, and active-living advocates who can partner on group-based content.

## Collaboration focus
- Daily check-in stories or reels showing the creator’s REAP survival journey.
- Honest content that celebrates consistency, acknowledges elimination risk, and invites followers to join the next season.
- Local community activations: guided group walks, open “survival squad” meet-ups, or co-hosted content with Sport Waikato partners.
- Referral incentives tied to limited early access, local prize draws, or season pass gifting for followers.

## Recommended shortlist
1. Waikato-based wellness coach or personal trainer with a strong local Instagram/Facebook audience and regular movement content.
2. Hamilton lifestyle creator who reaches adults balancing work, family and health, and who can frame REAP as a sustainable habit tool.
3. NZ micro-influencer specialising in low-impact exercise, walking, or Zone 2 training for everyday people.
4. Community-running or walking group organiser with an engaged local network and experience promoting events.
5. Mental wellbeing or habit coach who can contextualise REAP as a manageable 21-minute commitment and a behaviour-change experiment.
6. Workplace wellness influencer or HR wellbeing partner active in national NZ employee wellbeing communities.
7. Local health practitioner, physiotherapist or exercise scientist who can provide credibility and speak to REAP’s safe, evidence-based design.
8. Family-orientated NZ content creator who can show how REAP fits into busy household routines without needing gym gear.

## Selection criteria
- Audience size: ideally 5,000–50,000 followers with high engagement, or a smaller highly engaged local audience.
- Content quality: good video storytelling, clear messaging, and a tone that feels real rather than overly produced.
- Community trust: evidence of active comments, local event participation, or repeat series content.
- Relevance: consistent focus on movement, health, habits, community, or New Zealand lifestyle.

## Suggested outreach approach
- Open with Sport Waikato’s purpose: a charitable trust building a new wellbeing product with research and community impact at its core.
- Explain that REAP is a 30-day daily movement survival game where surviving participants earn eligibility for spot prize draws funded separately by Sport Waikato.
- Offer creators early access to the October soft launch cohort, plus content support and co-branded local storytelling.
- Emphasise that creators can be honest about the challenge: elimination is part of the narrative and is a strength, not a risk.

## Next steps
- Research and confirm 10–15 specific creator handles matching the shortlist.
- Draft personalised outreach messages for each creator category.
- Prepare a small local content kit: briefing, key messages, sample caption prompts, and a clear community offer.
- Track outreach responses and prioritise creators who can support REAP’s launch story with authenticity and local reach.
