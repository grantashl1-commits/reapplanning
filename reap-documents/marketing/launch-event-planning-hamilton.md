# Launch Event Planning (Hamilton)

**Prepared by:** Marketing Team
**Date:** 2026-04-09
**Status:** Draft

## Purpose
Design a premium Hamilton launch event for REAP that delivers a compelling media spectacle, deep partner credibility, and an immersive participant experience. The event will establish REAP’s mission-leadership, introduce early cohort participants, and generate national momentum for the October soft launch.

## Strategic objectives
- Create a flagship launch moment that positions REAP as a high-value Sport Waikato innovation.
- Deliver rich owned media assets for social, email, PR and partner storytelling.
- Activate local stakeholders including community partners, employers, health professionals and research collaborators.
- Recruit a visible early cohort of influencers, ambassadors, and Hamilton residents.
- Demonstrate the launch event as a catalyst for Year 1 registration and long-term national expansion.

## Event concept
Host an elevated Hamilton activation that blends credibility, theatre, and wellbeing.
The launch should feel like a professional health innovation showcase, not a gimmick.
- High-production live stage for the REAP reveal, with premium AV, hosted introductions, and curated content moments.
- A structured experiential floor where guests can sample the REAP journey, understand the daily movement requirement, and participate in live challenge activations.
- A credibility zone featuring expert voices from Sport Waikato, healthcare partners, researchers, and the celebrity/cohort ambassadors.
- A community hub for local employers, whānau groups and participant squads to enrol together.

## Event pillars
### 1. Main stage launch experience
- Premium keynote reveal of REAP’s mission, design rationale, and soft launch timeline.
- Short documentary-style film introducing the product, research insights, and participant profiles.
- Live conversation panel with Sport Waikato leaders, a health expert, and a national ambassador.
- Official countdown and registration call to action for the first season.

### 2. Immersive participant showcase
- Movement walkthrough stations that explain the 21-minute Zone 2 requirement, Redemption Days and survival rules.
- Real-time interactive demos that show how a participant records progress, receives feedback, and stays connected.
- A “survival lab” experience where attendees can test simple movement tasks, heart-rate education, and practical habit tools.

### 3. Media & social content ecosystem
- Dedicated press area with an event media kit, interview lounge, and rapid-response PR team.
- Professional photography and video capture of launch highlights, participant stories, and branded event moments.
- On-site content production booth for influencer and ambassador testimonial shoots.
- Live social streams and short-form content reels distributed during the event.

### 4. Partner & community activation
- Partner showcase pods for local employers, health providers, iwi/whānau organisations, and research collaborators.
- A hospitality lounge for funders, sponsors, and VIP community leaders to network and brief key staff.
- Group registration support for community cohorts and employer teams.
- A follow-up commitment wall for attendees to pledge their first REAP season.

### 5. Premium hospitality
- High-quality event catering focused on healthy, local, everyday food.
- Executive welcome and dedicated concierge support for VIP guests, media, and partners.
- Branded welcome packs that include a launch guide, participant briefing, key messaging, and a clear next-step path.

## Audience and guest list
### Core guests
- Sport Waikato leadership and strategic partners.
- Regional health, research and wellbeing stakeholders.
- Hamilton business and employer wellness leads.
- Local media and national health reporters.
- Influencers, celebrities and cohort ambassadors invited to join the soft launch.
- Early access registrants, community squad leaders, and participant sign-ups.

### Priority invitees
- Funders and partner organisations with national reach.
- Health and movement experts who can validate REAP’s design.
- Workplace wellbeing decision-makers for immediate employer cohorts.
- Hamilton community leaders who can extend the launch story into local networks.

## Venue and production requirements
- Use a high-profile Hamilton venue with a strong arrival experience, media capability, and accessible community capacity.
- Professional AV production for keynote, film playback, panel interviews, and live streaming.
- Branded experiential zones with premium signage, interactive displays, and on-brand environmental design.
- Seamless guest flow between stage, experience zones, networking areas, and hospitality.

## Promotion & invitation strategy
- Personalised launch invitations for media, partners and VIPs.
- Partner-led co-marketing through Sport Waikato networks, health partners, and employer channels.
- Social teasers and hero content seeded one week before the event.
- A high-impact launch announcement distributed across owned channels, PR, and paid amplification.

## Event timeline
### Pre-event build
- Confirm venue, production partner, talent and media brief.
- Finalise event script, launch film, presentation content, and experience design.
- Secure core attendees, partners, and ambassador commitments.
- Create press materials, content schedules, and hospitality planning.

### Event day sequence
- Welcome / arrival experience and VIP registration.
- Main stage launch with film, keynote, panel and official reveal.
- Experiential immersion sessions, partner tours and media interviews.
- Live content capture, influencer testimonials, and audience engagement moments.
- Closing registration call and invitation to join the first REAP season.

### Post-event follow-up
- Distribute a launch recap package to media and partners.
- Share a highlight film and social reels within 24 hours.
- Follow up with attendees for registration, cohort recruitment, and stakeholder commitments.
- Use event momentum to fuel the next three weeks of launch content and paid acquisition.

## Success measures
- National and local media coverage secured for the launch.
- Positive stakeholder endorsements from health partners and community leaders.
- Early access sign-ups and employer cohort commitments captured on the day.
- High-quality launch assets produced for sustained campaign use.
- Social engagement and content reach from influencers and ambassadors.

## Investment approach
This is a premium launch event built for scale and credibility.
Resources should prioritise premium production, professional content creation, strategic partner hospitality, and a launch experience that reflects REAP as a serious wellbeing innovation and Sport Waikato leadership moment.
