# Email Newsletter Series (8 pre-launch)

**Prepared by:** Marketing Team
**Date:** 2026-04-09
**Status:** Draft

## Purpose
Build an 8-email pre-launch campaign that positions REAP as New Zealand’s first movement survival game, secures early sign-ups, and establishes urgency around the launch.

## Strategy
- Lead with REAP’s unique proposition: a serious daily movement behaviour intervention with game-like accountability.
- Keep the tone polished, confident, and bold — the kind of campaign a billion-dollar brand would brief to its agency.
- Use strong social proof, mission-driven language, and clear calls to action.
- Support each email with a social hook or activation idea to amplify momentum.

## Core messaging
- REAP is the first product of its kind in New Zealand.
- 21 minutes of Zone 2 movement every day is evidence-based and achievable.
- The cost of not moving is the emotional and social loss built into the game.
- Sport Waikato is funding the prize structure separately from subscription revenue.
- This launch is a cultural moment — not just another fitness product.

## Email sequence and templates

### Email 1 — Welcome: "This is REAP"
Subject: "Welcome to REAP — your first movement game starts here"
Preview: "Not a gym app. Not a wellness plan. A movement survival game."

Hero message:
- Introduce REAP as a 30-day daily movement survival game from Sport Waikato.
- Explain the simple rule: complete 21 minutes of Zone 2 movement before midnight each day.
- Make the urgency clear: public launch is 1 November, and early access opens now.

Body copy:
- "REAP is not another tracker. It is a behavioural intervention designed to make movement matter."
- "If you miss a day, you are eliminated for that season. That is the accountability people actually need."
- "Every surviving active participant becomes eligible for spot prize draws — not because of luck, but because they showed up."

CTA:
- "Secure your early access spot"

Social support idea:
- Post a short hero video teasing the launch with the line: "This isn't a challenge. This is REAP."

## Campaign delivery notes
- Use clean, modern design with dark, bold visuals and high-contrast accents.
- Keep copy concise and assertive. Avoid gimmicks.
- Use social proof where possible: early sign-up count, local support, Sport Waikato backing.
- Align email tone with PR messaging: confident, responsible, and distinctly different from ordinary fitness campaigns.

## What to avoid
- Do not reintroduce a heavy 1990s theme in the email copy.
- Do not overpromise health outcomes or make specific medical claims.
- Do not describe the prizes as the main reason to join.
- Do not position REAP as a casual wellness project; it should feel intentionally serious.
