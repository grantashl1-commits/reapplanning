# Waikato Media Outreach Plan

**Prepared by:** Marketing Team
**Date:** 2026-04-09
**Status:** Draft

## Purpose
Outline a local media outreach plan to generate coverage for the Hamilton launch of REAP.

## Strategy
- Target regional outlets with a strong community and lifestyle focus.
- Position REAP as a Hamilton-first wellness initiative with a playful 1990s pixel identity.
- Leverage celebrity and local fitness partner stories to make the pitch more compelling.

## Target outlets
- Waikato Times
- RNZ Waikato
- TVNZ Waikato
- Newshub Waikato
- Hamilton lifestyle and community blogs

## Story hooks
- Hamilton’s new pixel fitness challenge for adults who remember arcade gyms and cassette mixtapes.
- Celebrity ambassador announcement and local community activation.
- Retro pixel event pop-up with an arcade-style launch party.

## Tactics
- Personalised media pitch emails with pixel-themed press kits.
- Offer interviews with the celebrity ambassador, Sport Waikato leader, and early beta participants.
- Provide high-quality images and 90s-inspired visuals for editorial use.
- Follow up with social media teasers aligned to the PR timeline.
