# Referral Incentive Programme Design

**Prepared by:** Marketing Team
**Date:** 2026-04-09
**Status:** Draft

## Purpose
Design a referral programme that turns word-of-mouth into a 1990s pixel-style crew-building game.

## Concept
- Encourage participants to "recruit your clan" like a retro multiplayer squad.
- Reward referrals with unlockable pixel badges, bonus entries, and exclusive early access.
- Use 90s-era references like mixtape shares, pager-style invites, and LAN party squads.

## Reward structure
- 1 referral: pixel badge and bonus encouragement message.
- 3 referrals: entry into a "Retro High Score" prize draw.
- 5 referrals: VIP early access and a physical retro pack (e.g. pixel sticker sheet, cassette-style card).

## Activation
- Provide sharable referral links with pixel-themed landing pages.
- Send email triggers styled like "You’ve got mail" but with a retro computer UI.
- Track referrals on a leaderboard displayed in the app.

## Messaging
- "Bring your crew. Survive the REAP together.".
- "Level up your referral streak.".
- "Collect your pixel rewards before the clock runs out.".
