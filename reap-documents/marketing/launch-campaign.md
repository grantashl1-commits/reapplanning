# REAP Launch Campaign
**Sport Waikato Incorporated | Premium Launch Strategy**

## Campaign Vision
Position REAP as New Zealand’s most ambitious movement intervention: a nationally visible, mission-driven behaviour change launch with the scale, production value, and partnership strength of a billion-dollar campaign.

REAP will not simply seek participants. It will create cultural urgency around improving adult movement, make Hamilton the epicentre of a proof-driven health experiment, and demonstrate Sport Waikato as the organisation capable of launching world-class behaviour change innovation.

## Campaign Objectives
- Secure 5,000+ paid early access registrations for Season 1 before public launch.
- Generate national media coverage across TV, radio, print, and digital outlets.
- Establish REAP as the definitive wellbeing challenge for everyday New Zealanders.
- Create owned content assets and case studies that underpin Year 2 national expansion.
- Build a deep referral engine through trusted community, corporate, and research partners.

## Strategic Pillars
1. Premium product launch narrative
   - Frame REAP as a highly credible, behaviour-change intervention, backed by Sport Waikato research and designed for real adults, not just athletes.
   - Use a ‘survive the month’ campaign narrative that is aspirational, inclusive, and evidence-based.

2. High-impact experiential launch
   - Deliver a multi-day flagship launch event in Hamilton featuring a live REAP experience, broadcast-quality content production, and partner showcases.
   - Create a physical activation that brings the survival game mechanics to life for local media and influencers.

3. Trusted partnerships & credibility
   - Mobilise government, health, academic, and corporate partners to amplify reach and legitimacy.
   - Use strategic endorsements from NZ sports figures, wellbeing experts, and regional leaders.

4. Story-led performance and community growth
   - Build a content engine around participant storytelling, elimination moments, and survivor achievement.
   - Drive sign-ups through emotionally engaging paid, earned, and owned channels.

## Audience Priority
- Primary: adults 30–55 in Waikato who are genuinely underactive and seeking a structured, compelling way to move more.
- Secondary: employers, workplace wellbeing leads, and community groups who can sponsor or recruit cohorts.
- Tertiary: national NZ health-conscious consumers receptive to research-backed, equity-aware movement programmes.

## Campaign Components
### 1. Flagship Launch Experience
- Hamilton launch showcase with live survivor activation, guided group warm-ups, and a branded media wall.
- Invite national media, key funders, Sport Waikato partners, local MPs, and identified influencers.
- Stream the launch event to a dedicated digital hub and social channels.
- Produce a cinematic launch film plus cutdowns for social, emails, and partner use.

### 2. Bespoke audience funnel
- Premium landing page with conversion-focused registration flow, countdown timer, proof points, and research callouts.
- Dedicated employer/corporate wellbeing landing page for group sign-ups.
- Regional partner pages for community group referrals and RST networks.

### 3. Content & storytelling campaign
- High-production hero films: “Survive the Reap,” “Why 21 Minutes Matters,” and “The Hamilton Challenge.”
- Participant mini-documentaries tracking early cohort journeys from signup to elimination or survival.
- Weekly cinematic social episodes that highlight survivor rituals, community squads, and wellbeing insights.
- CEO / Sport Waikato leadership profile content that explains the Living Lab rationale and funding independence.

### 4. Media & PR ecosystem
- National launch press release package targeted at TVNZ, Three, RNZ, NZ Herald, Stuff, Metro, and specialist health outlets.
- Local Hamilton/Waikato media package with community impact hooks, partner endorsements, and launch event invites.
- Expert opinion pitches on health behaviour, workplace movement, and the ethics of elimination-based gamification.
- Ongoing media cadence: launch announcement, partner reveal, celebrity soft launch progress, final countdown.

### 5. Influencer & ambassador activation
- Secure a national NZ celebrity ambassador for the October soft launch cohort.
- Recruit 10–15 premium influencers and micro-influencers to join the live launch event, document their REAP journey, and send followers to registration.
- Create high-value creator collaborations: workplace wellbeing takeovers, “survive with me” series, and community squad challenges.
- Package creator content for resharing across Sport Waikato channels and partner networks.

### 6. Paid acquisition & retention
- National paid social campaign across Facebook/Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and LinkedIn for workplace sign-ups.
- Targeted digital search and display for key audiences in Waikato, Auckland, Wellington, and Christchurch.
- Retargeting funnels for site visitors, content viewers, and early interest form leads.
- Cohort-based email nurture for warm leads, early access registrants, and corporate partners.

### 7. Community partner mobilisation
- Deep partnerships with local workplaces, health clinics, gyms, iwi organisations, and sports clubs.
- Field activation teams to deliver on-site demonstrations, sign-ups, and live Q&A sessions.
- Community ambassador toolkit with co-branded assets, briefing notes, and referral incentives.

### 8. Research & funder communications
- Launch a concurrent “REAP Research Narrative” that explains data collection, wellbeing outcomes, and community benefit.
- Share early launch metrics with Sport NZ, WEL Energy Trust, Waikato DHB, and research partners.
- Position the launch as the first stage of a scalable national Living Lab intervention.

## Launch Timeline
### Phase 0 — Pre-launch (4–6 weeks before open registration)
- Finalise launch creative, event production partners, and premium content assets.
- Confirm celebrity ambassador and influencer cohort.
- Build out landing page funnels, employer pages, and partner experience pages.
- Secure national launch media commitments and partner co-marketing agreements.

### Phase 1 — Announcement week
- Reveal REAP Season 1 with a bold launch broadcast and premiere film.
- Open registration with a limited early access offer and employer cohort incentives.
- Run the Hamilton launch showcase and distribute launch PR pack.
- Begin paid media amplification and partner-led local outreach.

### Phase 2 — Engagement build (weeks 2–4)
- Publish participant journeys, expert explainers, and “how to survive REAP” content.
- Activate local community events, workplace briefings, and partner sign-up drives.
- Maintain national media momentum with impact stories and ambassador progress updates.
- Use retargeting and email cadence to convert warm leads and keep early access urgency high.

### Phase 3 — Final countdown (week 5)
- Drive scarcity with “final cohort open” messaging and registration deadline reminders.
- Release survivor spotlights and elimination stories to fuel FOMO.
- Host a final live Q&A and movement rally in Hamilton.
- Lock in employer and community cohort commitments before the season starts.

### Phase 4 — Launch & first season support
- Share daily momentum content from the first season, including survivor boards, prize draw teasers, and community shoutouts.
- Provide continuing onboarding and support guides for participants.
- Capture launch metrics, behaviour-change signals, and media performance for the post-season report.

## Investment mindset

- world-class content production,
- a cinematic Hamilton launch experience,
- celebrity and influencer credibility,
- premium partner activations,
- national paid reach, and
- rigorous measurement for Year 2 national scale.

- Media impressions: 15M+ NZ audience reach

- Employer/corporate cohort sign-ups: 50+ teams
- Early cohort retention and active participation rates as launch proof points

REAP should emerge from launch as a recognised Hamilton innovation, a credible Sport Waikato behaviour-change product, and a national proof point for how a community-led wellness brand can combine premium production, research rigour, and tangible movement outcomes.
