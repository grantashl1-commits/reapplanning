# REAP Launch Campaign
**Sport Waikato Incorporated | Marketing Campaign**

## Campaign Goals
- Drive registration for the next REAP season.
- Communicate the simple daily movement rule.
- Reinforce the welfare-first and transparent prize model.

## Key Messages
- “Move 21 minutes a day. Survive the 30-day challenge.”
- “Two Redemption Days. Seven prize draws. One community.”
- “REAP is designed for real people, not elite athletes.”

## Campaign Components
- Landing page hero copy with the season countdown.
- Social posts featuring real survivor stories.
- Email sequence for new registrants and warm leads.
- Press outreach emphasising community health impact.

## Launch Timeline
- Week 1: announce season launch and open registration.
- Week 2: feature how-to content and support resources.
- Week 3: share testimonials from early registrants.
- Week 4: final push before the season starts.
