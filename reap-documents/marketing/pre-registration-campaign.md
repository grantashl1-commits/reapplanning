# Pre-Registration Campaign

**Prepared by:** Marketing Team
**Date:** 2026-04-09
**Status:** Draft

## Purpose
Build a high-impact pre-registration funnel that uses emotional psychology and persuasive email sequences to convert free waitlist sign-ups into committed REAP participants and long-term engaged users. The pre-registration phase is not just about capture—it is about psychological investment, identity formation, and anticipation.

## Strategic psychology framework
The REAP pre-registration uses five core psychological principles:

1. **Loss Aversion**: People fear missing out more than they desire gaining. Position waitlist spots as limited and the opportunity as non-renewable.
2. **Social Proof**: Show momentum, community, and the growing number of people committing to the REAP challenge.
3. **Identity & Belonging**: Help people see themselves as part of a movement to reclaim daily movement and wellbeing.
4. **Scarcity & Exclusivity**: Create tiers (early access, soft launch, Season 1) that make people feel they are "in the know."
5. **Anticipation & Reward**: Build momentum through a sequence of emails that reward sign-up and keep REAP top-of-mind.

## Campaign concept
**"Join the Movement Before It Moves Without You"**

Position REAP as a limited-window opportunity to be part of a health revolution. The emotional hook: *You've known for years you need to move more. This is the moment. This is the community. This is the last chance to be in before it fills up.*

- Subheading: "Sport Waikato is launching REAP in October. Join 5,000+ others who are committing now."
- Supporting line: *"Spots fill fast. Early access closes [DATE]. Don't miss it."*

  - "I've tried everything. This is the first habit that stuck." — Alex, Hamilton
  - "I thought I couldn't do it. Day 30 proved me wrong." — Jordan, Waikato
  - "Being part of a community of survivors changed everything for me." — Casey, Local

### Social proof elements
- Live counter: "4,247 people have joined the waitlist"
- Momentum messaging: "[X] new sign-ups this week — REAP is gaining traction"

### Limited availability messaging
- "Early access closes [DATE]. After that, you'll join the public waitlist."
- "Soft launch cohort seats: [X] remaining"
- "First 500 registrants get [Exclusive benefit]"

### Call-to-action sequence
- Primary CTA: "Secure Your Spot (It's Free)" — large, contrasting button
- Secondary CTA: "See What You'll Get" — explains the first email and what happens next
- Fear-based CTA variant: "Don't Miss Out — Join Now"

## Pre-registration form design
- Minimal friction: 3 essential fields only (name, email, zone code/location)
- Psychological micro-copy: "Join 4,000+ Waikato adults committing to change"
- Post-submission: immediate confirmation page with next steps and a sense of relief/celebration

## Email nurture sequence (12-email series over 12 weeks)

### Email 1: Welcome & Confirmation (sent immediately)
**Subject line (emotional trigger)**: "You're In. Here's Why This Matters."
- Celebrate their decision to join
- Explain what happens next (soft launch date, Season 1 launch, early access phases)
- Set expectations: "In the next 12 weeks, we'll help you prepare for the challenge ahead."
- Include a quick 2-minute video of Sport Waikato explaining REAP's why
- CTA: "Start your free preparation guide" (lead magnet)

### Email 2: Identity Formation (Day 2)
**Subject line**: "You Just Joined Something Different"
- Explain REAP's mission and why Sport Waikato built it (not for profit, for purpose)
- Show community commitment: "You're now part of 4,200+ people who believe daily movement is possible"
- Feature: participant quote about why they're joining
- Offer a downloadable "REAP Manifesto" or values statement they can share
- CTA: "Share with one friend who needs this" (builds social proof + referral loop)

### Email 3: Fear & Opportunity (Day 5)
**Subject line**: "Here's What You'll Be Up Against (And Why You'll Win)"
- Address the fear: "The 30-day challenge sounds hard. It is. But..."
- Reframe elimination: "Getting eliminated isn't failure—it's honest feedback. And you can try again next season."
- Share a soft launch story: someone who got eliminated on Day 8 but is trying Season 1
- Practical tip: "Start practicing your 21 minutes now" (habit-building)
- CTA: "Download the free 21-minute starter workout"

### Email 4: Social Proof & FOMO (Day 8)
**Subject line**: "Why [X] Companies Are Bringing REAP to Their Teams"
- Feature an employer spotlight: "How [Local Company] got their team to move more"
- Workplace participation creates FOMO: "Your workplace could have a team on the leaderboard"
- Show group benefits: team challenges, wellness credits, friendly competition
- Hint at corporate team prizes and recognition
- CTA: "Nominate your workplace for a REAP team pilot"

### Email 5: Personal Commitment (Day 12)
**Subject line**: "What's Your Why?"
- Ask: "Why do you want to move more? Tell us."
- Create investment: "This question matters because your answer is your motivation on Day 15."
- Collect responses for testimonial mining
- Explain that their "why" will be tied to their REAP journey
- CTA: "Share your story (we'll feature the best ones)" (investment + social recognition loop)

### Email 6: Zone 2 Education (Day 15)
**Subject line**: "21 Minutes of What? Let's Demystify Zone 2"
- Break the myth: "You don't need to suffer to move more. Zone 2 is sustainable, powerful, and right for you."
- Include simple Zone 2 explanation + heart rate calculator link
- Feature expert voice from Sport Waikato researcher
- Offer free Zone 2 training guide PDF
- CTA: "Calculate your Zone 2 heart rate range"

### Email 7: Scarcity & Urgency (Day 19)
**Subject line**: "Soft Launch Spaces: Only [X] Left"
- Create urgency: "The October soft launch starts [DATE]. Celebrity cohort is full. Influencer seats: [X] remaining."
- Explain tiers: "Early access participants get [benefit]. Public launch gets [benefit]. Which path are you on?"
- Social proof: show the list of confirmed soft launch participants (if appropriate)
- CTA: "Confirm your slot for early October access"

### Email 8: Eliminate Doubt (Day 23)
**Subject line**: "Can I Really Do This? (Yes. Here's Proof.)"
- Share a participant success story from someone like them
- Address objections: "I'm not fit enough / I don't have time / I'll fail"
- Counter each with evidence or a reframe
- Include a FAQ section
- CTA: "Still have questions? Book a 15-min call with our team"

### Email 9: Community Activation (Day 27)
**Subject line**: "Meet Your Fellow Survivors [Live Webinar]"
- Invite to a free webinar with Sport Waikato + 2–3 early participants
- Create belonging: "You're not doing this alone. Come meet your community."
- Q&A format, casual tone, live energy
- Exclusive webinar-only offer: discount on first month if they commit before [DATE]
- CTA: "Register for the live Q&A (Oct [DATE] at 7pm)"

### Email 10: Reciprocity & Value (Day 32)
**Subject line**: "Your Free Survival Kit (Before You Need It)"
- Deliver real value: "We've created a guide to your first week of REAP"
- Includes: habit-stacking templates, energy tips, how to deal with elimination, community resources
- Frame it as: "We're giving you this because we believe in your success"
- Personal touch: signed by a real team member
- CTA: "Download your Survival Guide"

### Email 11: Final Call & Deadline (Day 38)
**Subject line**: "[URGENT] Early Access Closes Tomorrow"
- Create final urgency: "After tonight, you move to the public waitlist. You'll still get in, but..."
- Reactivate FOMO: "Early access members get [benefit]. Public wait-list members get [benefit]. It's worth 24 hours of your time."
- Remove all friction: one-click upgrade link
- Short, powerful copy
- CTA: "Lock In Early Access Now"

### Email 12: Countdown & Anticipation (Day 45)
**Subject line**: "REAP Launches in [X] Days. You're Ready."
- Celebrate their registration: "You made the decision 6 weeks ago. Here's what's about to happen."
- 3-day countdown to Season 1 launch
- What to expect on Day 1 (login, survival board, daily check-in)
- Confirm their commitment: "You're all set. Let's do this."
- CTA: "I'm ready. Let's launch."

## Psychological hooks in email design
- **Subject lines** use curiosity, scarcity, social proof, and identity language
- **Sender name** is a real person (not "REAP Team" — use a name)
- **Personalization** uses first name, references their location or signup motivation if available
- **Visual design** uses calming, empowering colours (not jarring or aggressive)
- **Copy tone** is conversational, supportive, and emotionally resonant (not corporate)
- **CTAs** use behavioural language: "Secure," "Commit," "Join," "Claim" (not "Submit" or "Continue")

## Landing page copy deep-dive

### Hero section
- Headline: "Thirty days. 21 minutes daily. A community that survives together."
- Supporting copy: "REAP is a 30-day movement survival game. You move 21 minutes every day or you're out. Survive the month and you're eligible for seven prize draws. But the real prize is the habit."

### Why this matters section
- "The hardest part of moving more is starting. The second hardest is staying consistent. REAP removes both barriers—because daily accountability changes everything."
- Stat: "90% of people who join a community fitness challenge are more likely to maintain the habit 12 months later."

### Who is REAP for section
- "You've tried fitness apps. Gym memberships. Home workout videos. None of them stuck."
- "You know you need to move more. You just needed the right reason, the right community, and the right structure."
- "REAP is designed for people like you."

### What you get (early access benefits)
- "Early access members receive:"
  - Private email briefing before Season 1 launch
  - Access to free Zone 2 training resources
  - Exclusive community Discord channel
  - VIP ticket to the Hamilton launch event
  - First chance at employer team registration

### Objection handling
- **"I'm not fit enough"** → "REAP isn't about fitness. It's about consistency. 21 minutes at your pace. That's it."
- **"I'll get eliminated"** → "You'll learn what daily movement feels like. Elimination is data, not failure. You come back stronger next season."
- **"It costs too much"** → "Early bird pricing: $12/month or $120/year. Cheaper than a coffee a week. The investment in yourself is worth it."

## Referral mechanics (turning early adopters into advocates)
- **Referral loop**: "Invite a friend to REAP. For every person who joins with your link, you both get [reward]."
- **Rewards**: Bonus Redemption Days, early access to Season 2, exclusive merchandise
- **Viral coefficient**: Design for word-of-mouth—people want to invite their friends because it makes the experience better

## Success metrics
- **Pre-registration conversion rate**: target 15–20% of landing page visitors
- **Email open rate**: target 40%+ (emotional subject lines will drive this)
- **Email click-through rate**: target 10%+
- **Referral rate**: target 25% of registrants invite 1+ person
- **Retention on waitlist**: target 80% of registrants remain engaged until Season 1 launch
- **Upsell rate**: target 30% of early access tier conversion

## Post-registration nurture strategy
Once someone is registered, they are in a 12-week anticipation journey. The goal is to:
1. Deepen emotional commitment to the REAP mission
2. Reduce barriers to launch participation (education, preparation, community building)
3. Create social identity as a "REAP survivor" before Season 1 even starts
4. Build referral momentum (bring your friends, your team, your community)
5. Set up email list segmentation for paid acquisition targeting and retargeting

This is not transactional email. This is relationship building.
