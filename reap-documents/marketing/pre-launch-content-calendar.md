# Pre-Launch Content Calendar

**Prepared by:** Marketing Team
**Date:** 2026-04-09
**Status:** Draft

## Purpose
Build a 4-month pre-launch content calendar that leans into REAP’s 1990s pixel-art identity and nostalgic fitness challenge energy.

## Strategic theme
- Frame the campaign as a pixel-powered reboot of 1990s arcade culture.
- Use retro colour palettes, glitch effects, and 16-bit copy to feel authentic.
- Lean into the era when dial-up, mixtapes, MTV and pixel heroes ruled the screen.

## Content pillars
- "Press Start": early access and sign-up urgency for the most nostalgic health challenge ever.
- "Level Up": weekly fitness mission updates using 90s gaming metaphors.
- "Arcade Legends": participant stories told like high-score hall of fame entries.
- "Neon Nights": event and social visuals inspired by VHS, neon, and old-school gym flyers.

## Calendar structure
- Month 1: tease the world with pixel countdowns, mystery character reveals, and save-the-date messaging.
- Month 2: open pre-registration with early-bird challenge rewards and 90s mixtape-style email drops.
- Month 3: introduce celebrity pixel ambassador content and behind-the-scenes footage in retro TV style.
- Month 4: ramp into launch week with daily countdowns, arcade-style mini-challenges, and community share prompts.

## Tactics
- Weekly social posts with static pixel art, animated GIFs, and throwback captions.
- Bi-weekly email drops styled as "Floppy Disk Newsletters" with Easter egg codes.
- Influencer content featuring 90s wardrobe, retro workout moves, and pixel overlay branding.
- Local PR hooks tied to Hamilton’s cultural scene and retro pop-up activation.

## Deliverables
- Content calendar spreadsheet with campaign themes, channels, and publishing dates.
- Social creative briefs for TikTok, Instagram, and Meta.
- Email sequence outline with retro copy and visuals.
- Local PR outreach plan to support launch events.
