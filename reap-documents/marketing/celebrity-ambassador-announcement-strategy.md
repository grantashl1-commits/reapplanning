# Celebrity Ambassador Announcement Strategy

**Prepared by:** Marketing Team
**Date:** 2026-04-09
**Status:** Draft

## Purpose
Define a celebrity ambassador announcement that reinforces REAP’s survival challenge identity, local community relevance, and wellbeing-first positioning.

## Concept
- Present the ambassador as a trusted guide for the REAP journey, not just a celebrity face.
- Tie the announcement to the game’s main themes: resilience, daily movement, and community support.
- Keep the tone modern and energetic, with a subtle nod to nostalgic style in the visuals rather than the messaging.

## Announcement assets
- Short announcement video featuring the ambassador explaining why REAP matters and how the challenge works.
- Social posts that highlight the ambassador’s own fitness mindset, community roots, and support for wellbeing.
- Media copy that positions REAP as a Hamilton-led health initiative with an engaging new format.

## Channel plan
- Instagram Reel/TikTok: ambassador introduction + quick game rules + sign-up CTA.
- Facebook/Meta: a carousel that alternates ambassador story, how REAP works, and why local people should join.
- Local media pitch: focus on community wellbeing, the ambassador’s connection to Hamilton, and the game’s responsible design.

## Creative direction
- Use bold, playful pixel-inspired accents in the visuals, while keeping copy focused on the product and people.
- Avoid heavy 90s nostalgia in the announcement itself; use it only as a flavour in background graphics and badges.
- Show the ambassador as an active partner in the challenge: motivating participants, celebrating progress, and supporting safety.

## Execution priorities
- Make the celebrity announcement feel like the start of a community campaign, not a retro stunt.
- Keep the messaging simple: “REAP is the new way to move, compete fairly, and stay supported.”
- Ensure local media sees this as a wellness story with a strong creative hook, not just a themed launch.
