# Press Release — Soft Launch

**Prepared by:** Marketing Team
**Date:** 2026-04-09
**Status:** Draft

## Purpose
Draft the soft launch press release for the celebrity-backed REAP pilot in Hamilton, with a retro 1990s pixel identity.

## Angle
- Local sports and wellness innovation meets nostalgic entertainment.
- A Hamilton-based survival challenge built like a 1990s fitness game.
- Celebrity ambassador supports community activation and youth engagement.

## Key messages
- REAP is a new 30-day health challenge platform that blends wellbeing with game mechanics.
- Season 1 features 7 spot prize draws, welfare checks, and a $4,662 prize fund.
- The campaign embraces nostalgic 90s pixel graphics and throwback pop culture for a fresh, retro feel.

## Structure
- Headline: "Hamilton Gets a 1990s Pixel-Powered Fitness Survival Game"
- Lead: local celebrity, community wellness, and retro technology combine in a first-of-its-kind launch.
- Supporting info: launch dates, registration details, and celebrity ambassador involvement.
- Quote: from Sport Waikato and the celebrity ambassador, referencing nostalgia and fitness.

## Distribution
- Send to Waikato Times, RNZ Waikato, NZ Herald regional desk, and lifestyle outlets.
- Share through the brand’s social channels with downloadable press assets.
