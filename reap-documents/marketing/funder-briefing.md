# REAP Funder Briefing
**Sport Waikato Incorporated | Funder Strategy**

## Purpose
This briefing is for funders who want to back the first New Zealand product that combines proven behaviour-change science, community benefit, and a bold new cultural identity in physical activity.

## Executive summary
REAP is not a wellness campaign. It is a movement infrastructure product.

Built by Sport Waikato as a Living Lab intervention, REAP is a 30-day daily movement survival game that turns habitual activity into a collectible social behaviour and a fundable community asset. Participants commit to 21 minutes of Zone 2 movement every day. Failure to meet the daily requirement results in elimination from that season. Surviving participants remain eligible for seven spot prize draws, creating a promotional layer that enhances engagement without distracting from the product’s core mission.

This is a first-mover opportunity in New Zealand. No other funder-backed programme in the country is structured with this combination of:
- evidence-based daily movement targets,
- behaviourally anchored accountability,
- a community and cultural identity,
- research-grade data capture,
- reinvestment into charitable programming.

## Why fund REAP now
### 1. Brand power that cuts through
Sport Waikato is uniquely positioned to own this category. We are a trusted charitable trust with a proven community mandate, a Living Lab research mandate, and the credibility to run something uncomfortably different. REAP is designed to be bold, memorable, and hard for competitors to clone in terms of culture.

### 2. Behaviour change built on real science
REAP is aligned with World Health Organization activity guidance and explicitly designed around loss aversion: people are twice as motivated to avoid a loss as they are to gain an equivalent reward. The $13 subscription is the commitment device. The elimination mechanic is the accountability engine. The spot prize draws are a promotional bonus, not the primary product.

### 3. Community impact with funder accountability
Every funded dollar supports:
- community sport funding and regional wellbeing programmes,
- research capture and Living Lab reporting,
- affordable access for participants across Waikato,
- a transparent prize pool that is funded separately from subscription revenue.

### 4. First-mover cultural advantage
REAP will be the brand that introduces the elimination movement game to New Zealand. That first-mover position is powerful: a name, a phrase, and a community ritual can become cultural assets that cannot be bought after the fact. This is exactly the kind of initiative that funders want to back when they seek durable social impact.

## Funding model
- Participant subscription: NZD $13.00 per month.
- Season length: 30 days.
- Spot prize draws: seven draws per season, NZD $666 each.
- Prize funding per season: NZD $4,662.
- Annual prize commitment for four seasons: NZD $18,648.

Sport Waikato will maintain a strict separation between subscription revenue and prize funding. Prize funding is a funded promotional feature, not a pooled participant prize.

## What funders will unlock
### Scalable impact
Funding enables REAP to expand from a pilot season to multiple regional seasons, with a plan to reach 300+ participants by Season 2 and a platform-ready model for national RST adoption.

### Evidence generation
REAP is designed to generate the first real-world New Zealand dataset on daily-movement elimination behaviour, including:
- participation and survival rates,
- compliance patterns,
- welfare and support outcomes,
- movement behaviour changes over 30 days.

### Brand lift for funders
Supporters will be positioned as backers of the first product in New Zealand to make physical activity accountability meaningful at scale. This is a compelling narrative for brand, community, and impact reporting.

2. support for prize funding and promotional amplification,
3. introductions to partner organisations that can help recruit and support participants across Waikato.
