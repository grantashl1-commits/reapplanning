# REAP Funder Briefing
**Sport Waikato Incorporated | Marketing Brief**

## Executive Summary
REAP is a 30-day daily movement survival game that turns affordable participation fees into community impact and Living Lab research. The programme is designed to drive sustained behaviour change through clear rules, supportive messaging, and high-engagement prize draws.

## Funding Model
- Participant subscription: NZD $13.00 per month.
- Spot prize draws: seven per season at NZD $666 each.
- Total prize funding per season: NZD $4,662.
- Annual prize commitment for four seasons: NZD $18,648.

## Impact Narrative
REAP uses game design to make healthy movement a daily habit. The programme supports:
- physical activity across communities,
- mental wellbeing through structured daily goals,
- Living Lab evaluation of movement behaviour,
- youth and regional participation through affordable pricing.

## Ask
We are seeking funding and promotional support to scale REAP into additional seasons and expand recruitment across new communities.
