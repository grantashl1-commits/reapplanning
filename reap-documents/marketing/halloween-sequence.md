# REAP Halloween Sequence
**Sport Waikato Incorporated | Marketing Campaign**

## Campaign Concept
Use the Halloween season to promote REAP with spooky survivor imagery, daily movement challenges, and limited-time engagement incentives.

## Messaging Themes
- "Don’t let your survival streak die"
- "Stay alive through the haunted 30-day challenge"
- "Use your Redemption Days before the witching hour"

## Sequence
1. Pre-Halloween teaser: countdown to the season that keeps you moving.
2. Day 7 "Survivor in the dark" spotlight: share participant stories and encourage commitment.
3. Halloween week reminder: emphasise community support and the prize draw.
4. Post-Halloween follow-up: highlight how staying active is the real treat.

## Creative Notes
- Use dark, moody visuals with bright accents to signal action.
- Keep the tone playful, not scary.
- Include clear instructions on how to submit activity and use Redemption Days.
