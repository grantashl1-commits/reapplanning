# Video Testimonial Concept

**Prepared by:** Marketing Team
**Date:** 2026-04-09
**Status:** Draft

## Purpose
Create premium short-form video testimonials that capture the emotional reality of REAP: the fear, the commitment, the survival, and the community. Videos should feel intimate, authentic, and centre the human experience of daily accountability and shared resilience.

## Core Concept: Real Stakes, Real Stories

REAP videos are not motivational. They are documentary-like. They show:
- Participants at their most honest: struggling, pushing, surviving
- The daily 21-minute commitment as a real habit, not a highlight reel
- Elimination moments with raw emotion, not shame
- Community as the reason people come back
- The midnight countdown as a moment of genuine consequence

## Hero Video Series: "The Reaper Midnight"

### Cinematic Hook: The Reaper at Midnight
A signature visual/narrative element for REAP's biggest videos: the midnight moment when the reaper comes.

**Visual Treatment:**
- Dark, atmospheric cinematography showing a participant in their final moments before midnight
- Clock ticking down to 23:59 NZST
- A symbolic, shadowy presence (the reaper) moving through the scene — not gory or scary, but inevitable and respectful
- Real-time activity logging on screen: heart rate, movement, time remaining
- Midnight strikes — and the participant either crosses the threshold or is eliminated

**Emotional Journey (30–60 second version):**
1. Cold open: participant on Day 19 of REAP, exhausted but present
2. Their story (20 sec): "I've tried everything. Nothing stuck. Then REAP showed me that consequence matters."
3. The tension: Day 29, 11:47 PM. They have 13 minutes left. They're not at 21 minutes yet.
4. The push: they start moving. Real footage of a quick workout — no music, just effort and breathing
5. The midnight: clock hits 23:59. They've hit 21 minutes. The reaper passes. They survive.
6. Post-midnight reflection: "I didn't just survive a day. I survived the month. That's what changes you."

**Why this works:**
- The reaper is not a villain. It is structure. Respect for the mechanism.
- Midnight is the emotional climax — every participant can relate to the time-pressure moment
- Survival is visceral and real, not celebratory
- The video positions REAP as a serious commitment, not entertainment

### Video Testimonial Tiers

#### Tier 1: 15-Second Reels (Social / Paid Amplification)
**Format**: Tight, punchy cuts
**Content**: One emotional moment from a participant's REAP journey

**Variations:**
1. **The Pre-Midnight Moment**: Participant counting down their final minutes before the daily deadline, visible tension and focus
2. **The Elimination Story**: An honest moment from someone who got eliminated, explaining what they learned and why they're back for Season 2
3. **The Squad Accountability**: Two friends or coworkers checking in with each other at 11 PM, real banter, real stakes
4. **The Survivor Milestone**: Day 20 survivor reflecting: "I've never stayed consistent with anything for this long"

**Copy for each:**
- "11:43 PM. 18 minutes left. This is the moment that changes people."
- "I got eliminated Day 12. Best failure I've had. Season 2, I'm coming back stronger."
- "There's no one watching but each other. That's why it works."
- "20 days. I didn't think I could do this."

#### Tier 2: 30-Second Documentary (Hero Testimonials)
**Format**: Cinematic, interview-style
**Story structure**: Challenge → Commitment → Midnight Moment → Reflection

**Script Template:**
- **Opening question (voiceover)**: "Why did you join REAP?" or "What were you afraid of?"
- **Participant answer**: Real, unscripted response
- **Montage**: 10–15 seconds of the participant's daily life + movement moments
- **Midnight sequence**: The daily deadline moment (can be shot multiple times, use best footage)
- **Closing reflection**: What they learned, what's next

**Production Notes:**
- Shoot in participant's actual environment (home, workplace, park where they move)
- Capture real moments: fatigue, focus, relief, joy
- Midnight shots should show the clock on their phone/device, heart rate monitor, activity log
- If they get eliminated, capture that moment with dignity, not drama

#### Tier 3: 60-Second Narrative (Pre-Launch Hype)
**Format**: Full short film approach
**Content**: One participant's complete 30-day arc (or a portion of it)

**Story options:**
1. **The Comeback**: Someone who failed before, now surviving REAP for Day 20+
2. **The Squad**: Two or three friends surviving together, their accountability dynamic
3. **The Workplace Team**: Corporate team REAP season, workplace culture shift
4. **The Elimination & Return**: Raw honesty about getting culled, then committing to Season 2

**Production approach:**
- Shot over multiple days or seasons to capture authentic progression
- Include the participant's "why" at the beginning
- Multiple midnight moments to show the daily ritual becoming real
- Community elements (friends cheering, team messages, public board updates)
- Emotional arc: vulnerability → commitment → breakthrough

## The Reaper Midnight Mechanic in Video

The midnight cull is REAP's most distinctive moment. It should be featured prominently in testimonials.

### Visual Language for "The Reaper"
- Not a creature or character, but a presence — shadow, light, inevitability
- The reaper appears as:
  - A timer running down (clock-focused shots)
  - The final seconds of the day dissolving into darkness
  - A participant's anxious movement as they race to beat the clock
  - The moment darkness falls and a notification pings (participant eliminated or survived)

### Midnight Testimonial Moments (Best-Performing Engagement Points)
These 10–15 second clips can be extracted from longer videos and used independently:

1. **"The Panic"**: 11:47 PM, participant realises they haven't hit their target yet. Raw anxiety. They start moving.
2. **"The Relief"**: Midnight passes. They survived. Brief moment of visible relief.
3. **"The Reflection"**: Post-midnight, still breathing, reflecting on the moment
4. **"The Squad Moment"**: Two friends checking phones at midnight, seeing if they both made it
5. **"The Elimination"**: Notification comes through. Participant is culled. Honest reaction. Context: what they'll do next.

## Participant Selection & Casting

### Ideal Testimonial Participants
- **Authentic**: not fitness influencers, not people who've never struggled
- **Relatable**: employees, parents, people with real barriers to movement
- **Honest**: willing to show struggle, exhaustion, vulnerability
- **Diverse**: age, background, fitness level, reason for joining
- **Real outcomes**: mix of survivors, eliminated participants, Season 2 returners

### Recruitment
- Reach out to early October soft launch cohort
- Invite participation with full creative freedom
- Offer gift cards or free Season 2 as incentive (not payment)
- Protect privacy: all participants signed media release

## Distribution Strategy

### Vertical Formats (TikTok, Instagram Reels, Stories)
- 15–30 second cuts optimised for vertical viewing
- Strong opening hook (first 2 seconds must grab attention)
- Captions for sound-off viewing
- Clear CTA: "Join REAP" or "Register for Season 1"

### Horizontal Formats (YouTube, Paid Social, Landing Pages)
- 30–60 second testimonials
- High production value
- Embedded on landing page with conversion CTAs
- Paid placement on YouTube pre-roll and Facebook placement

### Email Integration
- Short testimonial clips embedded in launch countdown emails
- Participant stories in newsletter format
- Video testimonials in "Meet the Survivors" email sequence

### Social Media Calendar
- Daily Reels: one new 15-second clip per day (October soft launch period)
- Testimonial Wednesdays: longer 30–60 second full-story posts
- Midnight Mondays: focus on the midnight moment clips
- Weekly Stories: behind-the-scenes of shooting new testimonials

## Production Timeline

### Phase 1: October Soft Launch (Shoot & Post-Produce)
- Week 1–2: Scout participants, schedule shoots
- Week 2–4: Capture daily moments, midnight sequences
- Week 4–5: Post-production, edit short clips, longer testimonials
- Week 5–6: Begin releasing clips socially and in email

### Phase 2: November Launch (Feature & Amplify)
- Release final hero testimonials (30–60 second versions)
- Promote via paid social with 15-second hooks
- Embed in landing page and email sequences
- Build momentum for Season 1 registration

## Success Metrics
- **Video engagement rate**: target 8%+ engagement (average social video is 2–3%)
- **YouTube view rate**: target 40%+ watch-through rate on full testimonials
- **Social share rate**: target 5%+ of views shared (indicates emotional resonance)
- **CTR from video**: target 3%+ of video viewers click registration CTA
- **Referral attribution**: track how many participants came via testimonial video

## Creative Principles
1. **Authenticity over polish**: real struggle beats perfect production
2. **Midnight is the climax**: every video should build to the daily deadline moment
3. **The reaper is respectful**: consequence without shame
4. **Community is central**: even solo participants should feel the social stakes
5. **Elimination is not failure**: it's data. It's part of the story.
