## Overview

REAP Corporate is a group subscription offering for Hamilton/Waikato employers seeking measurable, accountable employee wellness programmes. Unlike passive wellness portals or one-off seminars, REAP Corporate puts employees in a daily accountability structure with real stakes — and gives employers an aggregate view of team activity without compromising individual privacy.

## 2. The Business Case

### 2.1 Why REAP Outperforms Traditional Workplace Wellness

| Factor | Traditional Programme | REAP Corporate |
|--------|----------------------|----------------|
| Engagement mechanism | Voluntary, passive | Daily survival stake — miss and you're out |
| Engagement rate (typical) | 15–35% of eligible staff | Target: 80%+ daily compliance in Week 1 |
| Measurability | Self-reported or aggregate steps | Wearable-verified Zone 2 heart rate |
| Employer visibility | Low — no useful data | Aggregate dashboard: team survival rate by week |
| Behaviour change duration | Often ends when programme ends | 30-day habit formation; research supports daily Zone 2 building lasting change |
| Cost | $50–$200/employee/year (typical) | $80–$120/employee for Season 1 (10 months at corporate rate) |
| Fun/social factor | Low | High — survival board, elimination tension, team competition |

### 2.2 The Science

Zone 2 heart rate training (moderate aerobic intensity, conversation-pace effort) has strong evidence supporting:
- Improved cardiovascular health markers
- Reduced metabolic syndrome risk
- Improved mood and reduced workplace absenteeism
- Sustainable long-term exercise habit formation

REAP requires 21 minutes/day — achievable by walking briskly to/from transit, a lunchtime walk, or a cycle commute. This is not elite fitness. It's daily movement.

### 2.3 The Cost of Inactivity

- MBIE estimates low physical activity costs the NZ economy $1.3–$2.5 billion annually in lost productivity and healthcare costs
- Waikato DHB data supports higher-than-national-average rates of lifestyle disease in the region
- ACC provides small business wellness incentives — REAP may qualify as a supported activity (see Section 15)

## 3. REAP Corporate Programme Structure

### 3.1 Group Registration

- Minimum 10 participants per corporate group
- Employer registers the group via REAP's corporate onboarding portal (simple CSV upload of employee emails or bulk invite link)
- Each employee receives individual REAP subscription invitation — they connect their own wearable device
- Employees opt in individually — employer cannot force registration (important for legal reasons — see Section 7)
- Billing: single invoice to employer, covering all group participants, monthly for the season duration

### 3.2 Corporate Dashboard

Employer has access to a read-only aggregate corporate dashboard showing:

| Metric | Visible to Employer | Visible Level |
|--------|---------------------|---------------|
| Total group participants registered | Yes | Aggregate |
| Total still surviving (day by day) | Yes | Aggregate count |
| Team survival rate (% of group still in) | Yes | Aggregate % |
| Weekly Zone 2 compliance rate (% of participants who logged each day) | Yes | Aggregate |
| Group ranking vs. other REAP corporate teams (if applicable) | Yes | Anonymous competitor comparison |
| Individual participant survival status | **No** | Individual data is never visible to employers |
| Individual participant health data | **No** | Individual data is never visible to employers |

### 3.3 Company League — Survival Board

- Each corporate group appears as a named team on the public REAP Survival Board
- Team name: agreed with employer (e.g., "Waikato DHB" or "Team Gallagher Basset" or anonymous at employer's preference)
- Public-facing: shows team size and survival rate — creates community and competitive spirit
- Employees can see their team's performance but cannot see each other's individual data through the employer view

### 3.4 End-of-Season Report

Delivered to the employer contact within 14 days of season close (by 14 December 2026).

| Report Section | Content |
|----------------|---------|
| Executive Summary | Team headline stats: participants, survivors, Zone 2 days achieved |
| Survival Curve | Day-by-day chart of team survival rate over 30 days |
| Activity Volume | Total minutes of Zone 2 logged across the team (aggregate) |
| Comparison | How team performed vs. Season 1 REAP average (anonymised) |
| Key Findings | 2–3 insight bullets from the data |
| Recommendations | Sport Waikato's recommendation for Season 2 or ongoing wellness investment |
| Data Privacy Statement | Confirmation of what data was and was not shared with employer |

### 3.5 CEO Challenge

Optional component: the CEO publicly registers as a REAP participant under their own name (or a recognisable handle), and employees can see the CEO on the Survival Board.

**Why this works:**
- Signals executive commitment to wellness — not just a HR poster on the wall
- Creates internal conversation and media opportunity ("Our CEO is trying to Survive the Reap alongside staff")
- If the CEO is eliminated, it's humanising — they failed the same challenge their team did
- If the CEO survives 30 days, it's a genuine achievement worth communicating internally and externally

REAP provides a CEO Challenge Pack: briefing document, suggested internal comms, and a co-branded "CEO Challenge" graphic for employer social channels.

## 5. Data Privacy Framework

### 5.1 What Employers Can See

Employers access only aggregate, group-level data through the corporate dashboard. No individual data is ever exposed to the employer.

### 5.2 What Remains Private to the Employee

| Data Type | Employer Access |
|-----------|----------------|
| Individual heart rate data | Never |
| Individual elimination date/status | Never |
| Individual activity logs | Never |
| Health metrics from wearable | Never |
| Whether specific employee is still surviving | Never |

### 5.3 Employee Consent

- Each employee subscribes individually and agrees to REAP's terms of service
- REAP's terms clearly state that aggregate data may be shared with the employer group administrator
- Employees who do not consent to any corporate dashboard visibility may request individual-only status (their subscription remains, they are excluded from corporate metrics)
- REAP complies with Privacy Act 2020 requirements — see T065 youth assessment for under-18 considerations

### 5.4 Data Storage

- All participant data stored in Supabase (NZ/AU region preferred; confirm with technical team)
- Corporate dashboard reads only from aggregated views — no raw participant table access
- Employer credentials are role-restricted: read-only aggregate view

## 6. Employer Liability Considerations

Employers should be made aware of the following before registering staff:

1. **Voluntary participation:** REAP is a voluntary wellness programme. Employers must not make participation mandatory or tie it to performance assessments. Ashleigh to flag this clearly in all corporate pitches.

2. **Physical activity clearance:** REAP recommends participants consult a GP before starting if they have known health conditions. Employers may wish to remind staff of this — particularly relevant in healthcare or physical roles.

3. **Exercise during work hours:** If employees exercise during work hours to meet their Zone 2 requirement, this is at the employer's discretion. REAP does not make representations about whether exercise during work hours is appropriate — that is the employer's policy decision.

4. **Elimination is not performance management:** Elimination from REAP is a game mechanic. It has no bearing on an employee's employment status, and employers must be absolutely clear that REAP elimination ≠ performance commentary.

5. **Health information:** REAP does not give individual health data to employers. If an employee discloses health information to their employer in the context of REAP participation, the employer should handle that through their normal HR channels.
