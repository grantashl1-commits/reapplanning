# T061 — Device Partner Negotiation (Garmin / Fitbit)
**Owner:** Ashleigh | **Due:** 2026-08-01 | **Priority:** High
**Status:** In Progress | **Target: Signed MOU by 1 August 2026**

---

## Overview

REAP uses TERRA as a unified wearable data integration layer. Device compatibility is already solved technically — this outreach is about **co-marketing and brand alignment**, not API dependency. The goal is to secure at least one named device partner before Season 1 launches, providing mutual brand credibility, potential subsidised device access for participants, and a NZ-first fitness survival game story that both Garmin and Fitbit/Google can leverage.

**Key principle:** REAP does not need these partnerships to function. This is an opportunity to amplify the launch and benefit participants. If no deal is reached, REAP proceeds as planned with TERRA.

## 4. Partnership Tiers

REAP can offer three escalating levels of partnership. Lead with Tier 2 in initial conversations; Tier 3 requires more internal resourcing and should only be opened if the device partner raises it.

| Tier | Name | What REAP Offers | What REAP Asks For | Effort |
|------|------|------------------|-------------------|--------|
| **Tier 1** | Co-Marketing Only | Logo in REAP app and marketing materials, mention in press release, device recommendation in onboarding | Social share of REAP content, press release co-sign, brand mention in partner's owned channels | Low — no technical work |
| **Tier 2** | Preferred Device Partner | All of Tier 1 + "Official Partner" badge, aggregated Living Lab data report post-season, ambassador wearable alignment | Device gifting/subsidy (20–50 units), joint press release, co-created content (1–2 pieces) | Medium — data report, asset coordination |
| **Tier 3** | Strategic Integration | All of Tier 2 + device-specific onboarding flow, featured placement in REAP app device selection, potential co-branded Season 2 | Sponsored devices for Season 1 participants (discount code), Season 2 co-investment conversation, potential API feature story | High — requires dev time; defer unless partner raises |

**Note on TERRA:** Do not promise any changes to REAP's device integration architecture. TERRA remains the technical backbone regardless of partnership tier. Any "integration story" is a marketing narrative, not a technical one.

## 8. Decision Criteria

### Proceed with partnership if:

- Device partner agrees to at minimum Tier 1 (co-branding + press release)
- No obligations are placed on REAP that conflict with TERRA integration or other device partners
- No requirement to exclude other devices from the REAP platform
- MOU signed by 1 August 2026 — sufficient lead time for Season 1 marketing
- No financial obligation required from REAP (cash payment to device partner)

### Decline if:

- Partner demands exclusivity that would require REAP to deactivate other device integrations
- Partner requires changes to REAP's technical architecture without funding the development
- Partner requires multi-year commitment before Season 1 data is available
- Negotiation timeline exceeds 1 August 2026 MOU deadline
- Any condition would compromise REAP's gambling compliance or charitable status
