## 3. Brand Briefing Document

### 3.1 REAP Brand Identity at a Glance

| Element | Specification |
|---------|--------------|
| Primary Name | REAP — Survive the Reap |
| Tagline | "Show up. Survive. Repeat." |
| Tone | Tough but supportive. Direct. Community-driven. Science-backed. Not aggressive or fear-based. |
| Visual Identity | Provided in separate Brand Guidelines PDF — primary dark palette with high-contrast accent, bold sans-serif |
| Organisation | Sport Waikato — NZ regional sport organisation, charitable purpose |
| Season | Season 1, launching 1 November 2026, Hamilton/Waikato, New Zealand |

### 3.2 Messaging DOs

- DO describe REAP as a "30-day fitness survival game" or "fitness challenge"
- DO explain Zone 2 as: "at least 21 minutes of moderate steady exercise — where you can still hold a conversation — every single day"
- DO emphasise community: "thousands of Waikato people all moving together"
- DO reference the daily commitment: "if you miss a day, you're out — so you have to make it count"
- DO talk about personal motivation and building a habit that lasts beyond the season
- DO use approved hashtags (see 3.4)
- DO tag @REAPwaikato (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok) in all REAP-related content
- DO refer to Sport Waikato when asked who runs REAP
- DO say "participants" or "survivors" — not "players" (avoids gambling connotations)
- DO mention the subscription: "$13 a month — less than a coffee a week"

### 3.3 Messaging DON'Ts

- DON'T describe REAP as a "competition to win money" or reference prize pool specifics without approved language
- DON'T use the word "bet," "wager," "odds," "jackpot," or "prize draw" in any context
- DON'T give medical advice or prescribe Zone 2 for specific health conditions
- DON'T guarantee health outcomes ("you WILL lose weight," "you WILL be fitter")
- DON'T share individual participant health data, even if visible in the app
- DON'T disparage competitors, other fitness programmes, or specific brands
- DON'T announce partnerships (Garmin, gym partners) before REAP issues a formal announcement
- DON'T speculate about Season 2 or future product features
- DON'T post content involving alcohol, gambling, or tobacco in the same post as REAP branding
- DON'T engage with negative or trolling comments publicly — escalate to Leanne

### 3.4 Approved Hashtags

**Primary (use in every post):**
`#SurviveTheReap` `#REAPwaikato` `#REAP2026`

**Secondary (use as appropriate):**
`#Zone2` `#Zone2Training` `#30DayChallenge` `#WaikatoFit` `#HamiltonFit` `#SportWaikato` `#FitnessHabit` `#EveryDayCounts`

**Do not create new REAP hashtags without approval from Ashleigh.**

### 3.5 Zone 2 Explanation for Social

Use one of the following approved explanations depending on format:

**Short form (caption/caption cutaway):**
> "Zone 2 is 21+ minutes of steady exercise — a brisk walk, easy run, swim, or cycle — where you can talk but it's a bit of an effort. Do it every day for 30 days. Miss one? You're out."

**Long form (video talking-head or interview):**
> "Zone 2 heart rate training is the sweet spot of aerobic exercise — moderate intensity where your body burns fat efficiently and builds your cardiovascular base. For REAP, that means at least 21 minutes every single day, tracked by your wearable device. The science is clear: consistent Zone 2 activity is one of the most effective things you can do for long-term health. REAP makes you do it every day, because the survival mechanic means there are real consequences if you don't."

## 8. Ambassador FAQ — Media and Public Questions

Prepare ambassador to answer these confidently and consistently. Briefing session recommended with Leanne before any media appearances.

### Q1: What is REAP?
**A:** REAP — Survive the Reap — is a 30-day fitness survival game run by Sport Waikato. You pay $13 a month to participate, and every day you must log at least 21 minutes of Zone 2 heart rate exercise — tracked by your wearable device. If you miss a day, you're eliminated at midnight. It's about building a daily movement habit with real stakes attached.

### Q2: What is Zone 2 heart rate?
**A:** Zone 2 is moderate aerobic intensity — the effort level where you can still hold a conversation but you're definitely working. Think brisk walking, easy jogging, cycling, or swimming. It's one of the most evidence-based exercise zones for long-term health. In REAP, 21 minutes of Zone 2 per day is the minimum to survive.

### Q3: Is this gambling?
**A:** No. REAP has been carefully designed with legal advice to ensure it does not meet the definition of gambling under the Gambling Act 2003. Participants pay a fixed subscription fee — $13 a month — regardless of outcome. There is no prize pool funded by entry fees. Sport Waikato, as a charitable organisation, has structured the programme as a fitness product, not a wagering product. [If pressed on prize structure: refer all questions to Leanne — do not speculate.]

### Q4: Why $13 a month?
**A:** It's deliberately accessible — less than a coffee a week. Sport Waikato's mission is to get more people in the Waikato region moving. The subscription funds the platform, the technology, and the programme. It's not about revenue; it's about commitment and accountability.

### Q5: What devices work with REAP?
**A:** REAP connects to most major wearable devices through an integration platform called TERRA. That includes Garmin, Fitbit, Apple Watch, Polar, Wahoo, and others. If you have a heart rate capable wearable, chances are it works.

### Q6: Why did you get involved?
**A:** [Ambassador to personalise — REAP team will draft suggested talking points aligned with ambassador's known motivations. Leanne to complete before onboarding meeting.]

### Q7: What happens if you miss a day?
**A:** You're out. That's the point. The elimination mechanic is what makes REAP different from a normal fitness app. It creates accountability — you can't skip and catch up tomorrow. But the game doesn't punish you as a person; it's designed to help you build the habit so missing a day becomes something you genuinely don't want to do.

### Q8: Is this only for fit people?
**A:** No. Zone 2 at the minimum level is a brisk 21-minute walk. REAP is designed for anyone who can maintain moderate exercise — it's inclusive, not elite. The challenge is the daily consistency, not the intensity.

### Q9: Is there a Season 2?
**A:** Sport Waikato is focused on making Season 1 the best experience possible. I'll let them speak to future seasons — that's Leanne's call, not mine.

### Q10: What if someone has a health condition?
**A:** REAP recommends anyone with a health condition consult their GP before participating — as with any exercise programme. The platform has safety prompts for this. I'm not a medical professional and I can't give medical advice.

## 12. Issue Escalation Protocol

### 12.1 Scenario: Ambassador Posts Off-Brand or Inaccurate Content

**Immediate response (within 1 hour of REAP team becoming aware):**

1. Ashleigh screenshots and archives the post with timestamp
2. Ashleigh notifies Leanne immediately by phone — do not email
3. Leanne assesses severity (see scale below)
4. Leanne contacts ambassador's primary contact (not publicly, not on social media) by phone
5. Agree on resolution: edit caption / delete post / post correction / no action
6. If post is deleted or corrected: log outcome in contract register, no further action unless pattern
7. If ambassador refuses to act: escalate to Sport Waikato CEO within 2 hours

**Severity Scale:**

| Level | Description | Response |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| 1 — Minor | Wrong hashtag, missing tag, slightly off-message | Ashleigh requests edit via DM; resolved within 24 hours |
| 2 — Moderate | Inaccurate claim about REAP (wrong price, wrong format) | Leanne contacts ambassador directly; edit or clarification post within 4 hours |
| 3 — Serious | Gambling language, medical claims, privacy breach | Leanne contacts ambassador AND their agent; delete within 2 hours; formal written notice |
| 4 — Critical | Content causes reputational damage to Sport Waikato | Leanne + CEO immediate response; legal adviser engaged; deletion required; review of contract termination rights |

### 12.2 Scenario: Ambassador Receives Media Request Without Prior Notice

1. Ambassador notifies Leanne immediately (before accepting or declining)
2. Leanne advises: accept / decline / accept with spokesperson support
3. If accepting: Leanne provides talking points and attends or is briefed in full
4. Ambassador does not give interviews about REAP without Leanne's prior approval
