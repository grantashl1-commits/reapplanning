**Owner:** Ashleigh | **Due:** 2026-09-01 | **Priority:** Medium
**Status:** In Progress | **Target: 3 signed gym partners before 1 November 2026**

---

## Overview

The REAP Gym Partnership Programme creates a two-way referral network between REAP and Waikato fitness centres. Gyms provide promotional reach and member discounts for REAP participants; REAP provides in-app promotion, Living Lab data insights, and a community of highly engaged, habit-focused members. The programme targets 5–10 gyms across Hamilton and the broader Waikato region, with a minimum of 3 signed partnerships before Season 1 launch on 1 November 2026.

**KPIs:**
- 3 signed gym partners before 1 November 2026
- 50+ REAP sign-ups attributable to the gym channel during Season 1
- 5 gyms in active outreach pipeline by 1 September 2026

## 3. Partnership Tiers

### Tier 1: Referral Partner

**Suitable for:** Any gym willing to display REAP materials without a formal discount arrangement.

| REAP Gives | Gym Gives |
|-----------|-----------|
| REAP Partner logo in app | REAP poster displayed at front desk |
| Marketing mention (social) | 1 social share at launch |
| QR code toolkit | Staff brief (1-page FAQ) |
| — | Basic verbal promotion to members |

**Formalisation:** Email confirmation. No formal MOU required. Easy to activate at scale.

---

### Tier 2: Promo Partner

**Suitable for:** Gyms willing to offer a member discount to REAP participants.

| REAP Gives | Gym Gives |
|-----------|-----------|
| All Tier 1 benefits | All Tier 1 contributions |
| Prominent "REAP Promo Partner" badge in app | 10% discount on casual gym access for REAP participants (verified via REAP app proof of subscription) |
| Featured in REAP onboarding "Local Gym Partners" section | 2 social posts per season (launch + mid-season) |
| Agreed gym logo in REAP app and website | Email promotion to gym's own member list (minimum 1 email) |
| — | Poster + QR code in changing rooms as well as front desk |

**Participant discount mechanism:**
- REAP participant shows REAP app subscription screen at gym reception
- Gym staff apply discount per their internal POS — no API integration required
- REAP provides a printable "REAP Partner Validation Card" for gym staff reference
- Discount applies to: casual visits, short-term memberships (1-month), and joining fee waiver where gym is willing

**Proposed discount:** 10% off first month's membership or casual visit rate. Gyms set their own floor — REAP does not mandate a specific dollar amount.

---

### Tier 3: Active Partner

**Suitable for:** Gyms willing to host events and engage more deeply with the Living Lab.

| REAP Gives | Gym Gives |
|-----------|-----------|
| All Tier 2 benefits | All Tier 2 contributions |
| "Sport Waikato Living Lab Partner" co-branding | Host 1 REAP Zone 2 Testing Event during Season 1 (Oct or Nov 2026) |
| Post-season aggregate data insights report (de-identified, consented) | Staff trained on Zone 2 basics to support REAP participants |
| Named in Sport Waikato media release | Dedicated space for REAP event (minimum 20 participants) |
| Season 2 priority partnership consideration | Access to REAP data story for their own marketing use (with REAP approval) |

**Zone 2 Testing Event:** A 60-minute session where prospective or new REAP participants test their Zone 2 heart rate threshold using simple walk/run protocol. Hosted at gym, facilitated by REAP team or qualified gym trainer. Generates content, community, and sign-ups.

### Gym Manager FAQ

**Q: What is REAP?**
A: A 30-day fitness survival game run by Sport Waikato. Participants track Zone 2 heart rate daily with a wearable device. It launches 1 November 2026 in Hamilton/Waikato.

**Q: What does this cost our gym?**
A: Nothing financially. You display a poster, brief your front desk, and optionally offer a participant discount. In return, REAP promotes your gym to our participants.

**Q: How many REAP participants will there be?**
A: We're targeting 1,000+ participants for Season 1 — predominantly Hamilton/Waikato residents. These are active, health-engaged people — your target demographic.

**Q: How does the participant discount work?**
A: REAP participants show their subscription screen on the REAP app. Your staff apply your standard discount procedure. No technology integration needed.

**Q: Do we need to do anything technical?**
A: No. This is a marketing partnership, not a technical one.

**Q: What does "Living Lab" mean?**
A: Sport Waikato runs a Living Lab innovation programme — we study health behaviour at scale. Active Partners receive aggregated, de-identified data about their members' Zone 2 activity patterns post-season. No individual data is shared.

**Q: Is this legal and legitimate?**
A: Yes. REAP is a Sport Waikato programme, a registered regional sport organisation in New Zealand with charitable status. The programme has received legal review.

**Q: How long does the partnership last?**
A: Season 1 is November 2026. The MOU covers Season 1 only. Both parties can renew for Season 2 if the relationship works well.

## 8. Co-Branding Guidelines

### Gym Logo in REAP App

- Gym logo displayed in the "REAP Partners" section of the app (Promo and Active tiers)
- Logos must be supplied by the gym as PNG or SVG, minimum 500px wide, transparent background
- REAP will not alter gym logos without written permission — scaling only
- Logos displayed at equal size with REAP Partner badge beneath
- Gym logos do not appear in REAP's primary navigation or above-the-fold app screens

### REAP Logo in Gym Materials

- Gym may display the REAP logo on their social posts and in-house screens using the approved asset from the REAP brand pack
- Gym may not alter REAP's logo (colour, shape, proportions)
- Gym must not use the REAP logo in a context that implies REAP endorses the gym's services beyond the agreed partnership
- Any co-branded materials created by the gym must be approved by Ashleigh before publication
