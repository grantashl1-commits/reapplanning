## Overview

REAP Season 1 requires earned media coverage to achieve its 1,000+ participant target without spending heavily on paid advertising. A media partnership — combining editorial coverage, social amplification agreements, and exclusivity relationships — can deliver significant reach at low cost, while generating the kind of third-party credibility that paid ads cannot.

The strategy prioritises **two or three deep relationships** over broad scatter-gun press releases. The celebrity ambassador announcement is the primary media hook. The elimination mechanic is the ongoing editorial story.

**Coverage KPIs:**
- Target earned media value: $20,000+ equivalent during Season 1
- Target reach: 50,000+ Hamilton/Waikato unique impressions from earned media
- Minimum 3 editorial pieces (not advertorial) across Season 1
- 1 exclusive media partner with ongoing season access
- 1 TV or video feature before or at Season 1 launch

## 2. Partnership Models

Three models are available. Select the right model for each outlet based on their editorial independence, commercial interests, and audience alignment.

### 2.1 Editorial Coverage (No Payment)

**Best for:** Waikato Times, Stuff Waikato, Hamilton Press

REAP provides information, access, and story angles. The outlet writes independently. REAP does not pay and does not control copy.

**Pros:** Highest credibility; public trusts editorial over advertorial
**Cons:** No control over angle; story may not run; timing uncertain
**What REAP provides:** Press releases, media briefings, exclusive access offers, spokesperson availability, data stories

### 2.2 Paid Advertising + Editorial Partnership

**Best for:** Waikato Times, The Base FM, More FM

REAP buys some advertising (modest budget) and in return negotiates editorial/promotional coverage alongside the ad package. Many regional media outlets have formal "partnership packages" that bundle advertising with editorial features.

**Pros:** More reliable coverage; guaranteed placements; advertising has direct conversion value
**Cons:** Cost; editorial coverage in this context may be labelled "sponsored" (less credibility)
**Budget guideline:** $2,000–$5,000 total paid media budget for Season 1 across all outlets. Ashleigh to allocate from REAP marketing budget.

### 2.3 Co-Production / Exclusive Access Partnership

**Best for:** One primary media partner (Waikato Times or Stuff Waikato preferred)

REAP offers **exclusive access** to Season 1 data and elimination feed in exchange for ongoing editorial commitment. The media partner gets a first-look story; REAP gets consistent editorial presence across the season.

**Pros:** Deepest coverage; ongoing story arc; media partner invests in the story
**Cons:** Exclusivity limits other media relationships for that specific angle; requires active media management throughout season
**What the exclusive includes:** First access to elimination statistics by day, interview access to Leanne/Ashleigh, ambassador interview coordination, end-of-season data story

## 4. Celebrity Ambassador as News Hook

The ambassador announcement is REAP's most valuable earned media asset before launch. Timing and execution:

### 4.1 Announcement Strategy

| Decision | Recommendation |
|----------|---------------|
| When to announce | **Late October 2026** — close enough to launch to create urgency, early enough to allow interview scheduling |
| Embargo strategy | Brief 1–2 trusted journalists under embargo 48 hours before announcement — they write the story ahead of time for simultaneous release |
| Press release timing | Issue at 9:00 AM on the announcement day for maximum pickup |
| Visual asset | High-res shoot images (from T060 brief) released simultaneously with press release |
| First interview | Leanne and/or ambassador available for interviews on announcement day — schedule 3–4 media commitments in advance |

### 4.2 Exclusive Announcement Opportunity

Offer the **Waikato Times or Stuff Waikato** a 24-hour exclusive on the ambassador announcement — they get the story first, run it the night before or morning of the general announcement.

In return: the outlet commits to editorial coverage at least twice during Season 1 (launch + mid-season or final).

### 4.3 Ambassador-Specific Media Considerations

- Ambassador appearance in media requires Leanne's prior approval for each booking (see T060 Section 12)
- Ashleigh prepares a media briefing pack for each booked interview
- Ambassador media engagements should be clustered around launch week and Season 1 Week 1 — not dispersed across the full 30 days
- Ambassador's own social media posts (T060) should align with media interview timing for maximum amplification

## 5. Exclusive Media Partner Offer

### 5.1 What REAP Offers the Exclusive Partner

- First access to daily elimination statistics (day-by-day survival rate data — aggregate, no individual names unless consented participants agree to be named)
- Access to Leanne and Ashleigh for minimum 3 editorial interviews during Season 1
- Ambassador interview coordination — one exclusive ambassador interview per season
- Real-time data dashboard access: exclusive partner can view season statistics before they are released publicly
- Right of first publication for the end-of-season data story ("Season 1 results")

### 5.2 What REAP Asks of the Exclusive Partner

- Minimum 3 editorial pieces during Season 1 (news, feature, or data story — not advertorial)
- Social amplification: minimum 3 shares of REAP content to outlet's social channels
- Cross-promotion: at minimum one mention of REAP's sign-up link in editorial content
- No negative or investigative coverage of REAP launched without giving Leanne 24-hour response right (standard media fairness — not censorship)

### 5.3 Exclusivity Scope

The exclusivity is **for Season 1 editorial content specifically** — it does not prevent other media covering REAP. It gives the exclusive partner a 48-hour head start on data stories and ambassador interviews.

## 8. Interview Preparation — Leanne and Ashleigh

### 8.1 Key Messages (Bridging Messages for All Interviews)

1. **The accountability message:** "REAP works because it creates a real consequence for missing a day. No other fitness product does that at this price point."
2. **The science message:** "Zone 2 heart rate training is one of the most evidence-backed exercise modalities for long-term health. REAP makes it the daily non-negotiable."
3. **The community message:** "This is about thousands of Waikato people all committing to move every single day in November. It's a community movement, not just a product."
4. **The charitable mission message:** "Sport Waikato is a charitable regional sport organisation. Our mandate is to get more people in the Waikato moving. REAP is a new way to do that."
5. **The innovation message:** "REAP is a first in New Zealand — and probably one of the first in the world at this scale. It's a genuine behavioural innovation."

### 8.2 Anticipated Interview Questions and Recommended Answers

**Q: Is REAP gambling?**
A: "No. REAP is a fitness subscription — $13 a month, fixed price, regardless of how long you survive. There's no prize pool, no betting, and no wagering. We've had legal advice on this. It's a fitness product with a survival game mechanic."

**Q: What happens if someone can't exercise for medical reasons?**
A: "REAP participants are encouraged to get medical clearance before starting, just as they would with any exercise programme. If someone is genuinely medically unable to participate, our refund policy covers that situation. Zone 2 at the minimum level is a 21-minute brisk walk — we've deliberately kept the bar accessible."

**Q: What do participants actually win?**
A: [Follow approved talking points — do not speculate on prize structure in media without Leanne confirmation. If asked: "The game is about the habit and the community — being one of the survivors at the end of 30 days. Sport Waikato is focused on making Season 1 the best experience possible — I'll let the programme details speak for themselves."]

**Q: How many participants do you expect?**
A: "Our Season 1 target is 1,000+ participants across Hamilton and the Waikato region. We're confident in that number based on the interest we've seen."

**Q: What if the technology fails and someone is wrongly eliminated?**
A: "We have a clear appeals process — if a participant believes they've been incorrectly eliminated due to a technical issue, they can contact REAP support within 24 hours. We have a technical review process, and where a fault on our side can be demonstrated, we reinstate the participant."

**Q: Is this just for fit people?**
A: "Absolutely not. Zone 2 at the minimum is a brisk 21-minute walk. REAP is for anyone who wants to build a daily movement habit. You don't need to be an athlete."

### 8.3 What Not to Say

| Don't Say | Instead |
|-----------|---------|
| Any specific prize pool dollar amounts not yet approved | "Sport Waikato is focused on making Season 1 the best experience possible — let the programme speak for itself" |
| Specific participant numbers that haven't been met | Stick to "target" framing |
| Medical endorsements ("this will cure you of...") | "The evidence base for Zone 2 training is well established — we recommend people consult their GP" |
| Competitor criticism | Stay positive and product-focused |
| Speculative Season 2 details | "We're focused on Season 1 right now" |
| Anything that confirms or denies whether specific named individuals are REAP participants | Privacy — "I can't comment on individual participants" |
