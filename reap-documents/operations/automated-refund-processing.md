# Automated Refund Processing

**Prepared by:** Dev Team
**Date:** 2026-04-09
**Status:** Draft

## Purpose
Document the automated refund process for participants who are eliminated or voluntarily exit.

## Requirements
- Calculate pro-rata refunds based on season progress
- Trigger Stripe refund events
- Record refund decisions and audit trail
- Provide admin controls for review and overrides

## Acceptance criteria
- Refund calculation rules documented
- Stripe refund workflow specified
- Approval and audit controls identified
