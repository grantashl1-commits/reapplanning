## Overview

This document establishes REAP's financial reporting requirements, including monthly reconciliation, quarterly analysis, and annual budgeting. Financial transparency is critical for Sport Waikato board oversight and regulatory compliance.

---

## Financial Reporting Principles

1. **Monthly reconciliation:** All revenue and expenses verified within 7 days of month-end
2. **Cash-basis accounting:** Revenue recognized when payment received; expenses recognized when incurred
3. **Transparency:** All reports reviewed by CEO + Finance Lead; board-ready within 10 days
4. **Audit trail:** Every transaction traceable to source (invoice, receipt, bank statement)
5. **Regulatory compliance:** Aligned with NZ Generally Accepted Accounting Practice (NZ GAAP)

## Key Financial Metrics (KPIs)

**Track monthly:**

| KPI | Formula | Target | Alert Threshold |
|-----|---------|--------|-----------------|
| Gross margin | (Revenue - COGS) / Revenue | 50% | < 40% |
| Customer acquisition cost | Marketing spend / New subscribers | $8 | > $15 |
| Subscription churn | (Cancelled) / (Start subscribers) | <5% | > 8% |
| Refund rate | Refunds / Subscriptions | <3% | > 5% |
| Expense ratio | Operating exp / Revenue | 40% | > 50% |
| Cash runway | Cash on hand / Monthly burn | 12+ months | < 6 months |
