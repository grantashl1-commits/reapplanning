# REAP — Competition Rules
**Sport Waikato Incorporated | Legal Draft**

## 1. Overview

These Competition Rules explain how REAP spot prize draws operate. They are part of the participant-facing rules for REAP seasons and are designed to make the prize draw process clear, fair, and legally defensible.

## 2. Prize Structure

1. REAP runs seven spot prize draws per 30-day season.
2. Each prize draw awards NZD $666.
3. Total prize funding per season is NZD $4,662.
4. Total annual prize commitment for four seasons is NZD $18,648.

## 3. Eligibility

1. Eligible entrants are active, confirmed-paid survivors at the draw trigger point.
2. A confirmed-paid survivor is a participant whose subscription payment has been received and who has not been eliminated from the current season.
3. No separate entry or additional payment is required to enter a prize draw.
4. Prize draw eligibility is automatic and contingent on season status.

## 4. Draw Schedule

The seven prize draws are scheduled as follows:
- Draw 1: Day 7 — Week 1 survivor
- Draw 2: Day 13 — Friday the 13th survivor
- Draw 3: Day 14 — Fortnight survivor
- Draw 4: Day 21 — Three-week survivor
- Draw 5: Day 24 — Redemption Day survivor
- Draw 6: Day 28 — Final week survivor
- Draw 7: Day 30 — Season finale survivor

## 5. Selection Process

1. Winners are selected at random from the eligible pool of confirmed-paid survivors.
2. The draw process is recorded, including the eligible pool size, winner identifier, draw timestamp, and selection criterion.
3. Prize winners are notified privately before any public announcement.
4. Winners must be 18 years or older and reside in New Zealand at the time of the draw, unless otherwise required by law.

**Draw Record-Keeping:** Sport Waikato maintains detailed records for each draw using the [Prize Draw Record Template](../technical/prize-draw-record-template.md). All records are retained for seven years to support regulatory compliance and dispute resolution.

## 6. Prize Funding and Compliance

1. Prize funding is maintained separately from subscription revenue.
2. No portion of participant subscription fees is used to fund prize awards.
3. The spot prize draws are promotional benefits attached to the REAP game. They are not separate paid-entry competitions.
4. Sport Waikato may adjust scheduling or procedures to comply with legal and regulatory requirements.

## 7. Disputes and Decisions

1. Sport Waikato's decision on prize eligibility and winner selection is final.
2. No correspondence will be entered into regarding draw outcomes, except as required by law.
3. **Replacement Winner Process:**
   - If a prize winner cannot be contacted within 10 business days of notification, Sport Waikato may select a replacement winner from the eligible pool.
   - If a prize winner is found to be ineligible (e.g., under 18 years old, not a NZ resident, or payment failed), a replacement winner may be selected immediately.
   - At least two contact attempts must be made via email and SMS before declaring a winner unreachable.
   - All replacement winner selection attempts and outcomes must be documented in the Prize Draw Record (see [Prize Draw Record Template](../technical/prize-draw-record-template.md)).
   - The replacement winner is subject to the same eligibility requirements as the original winner.
   - If no eligible replacement can be identified, the prize may be withheld or credited to the prize fund for the next season at Sport Waikato's discretion.
