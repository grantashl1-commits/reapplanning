# Consumer Guarantees Act 1993 Review for Survive the Reap

**Prepared by:** Shelley  
**Date:** April 9, 2026  
**Status:** Draft

## Purpose
This review assesses how the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993 and Fair Trading Act 1986 apply to Survive the Reap (REAP) subscription services, digital content delivery, refunds, and participant communications.

## Scope
- Subscription services delivered via survivethereap.nz
- Spot prize incentive structure
- Elimination and refund mechanics
- Terms and Conditions and marketing claims

## Key CGA Obligations

### Supply of services
Under CGA, services must be:
- Provided with reasonable care and skill
- Fit for the particular purpose made known by the consumer
- Delivered within a reasonable time
- Charged at a reasonable price

### REAP application
- **Reasonable care and skill:** REAP must maintain the app, wearable integration, and movement validation.
- **Fitness for purpose:** The service must support the participant’s daily Zone 2 movement tracking and elimination notification.
- **Reasonable time:** Customer support, refund processing, and medical hold decisions must be timely.
- **Reasonable price:** $13/month must align with what is reasonably expected for a guided movement challenge with insurance, welfare support, and prize incentives.

## Fair Trading Act Considerations

### Misleading or deceptive conduct
REAP marketing and onboarding must not:
- Misrepresent elimination probabilities
- Describe spot prizes as guaranteed rewards
- Imply the game is a gambling lottery rather than a promotional competition
- Overstate wearable accuracy or safety guarantees

### False representations
All communications must make clear:
- The competition format
- Total prize pool and draw schedule
- Refund eligibility and elimination risk
- Participant responsibilities for tracking movement

## Consumer Rights

### Refund rights
- Consumers may request a refund if the service is not provided with reasonable skill
- Voluntary exit and elimination refunds are managed as commercial policy, not automatic CGA entitlement
- Refund policy must be transparent and easy to find

### Guarantee disclaimers
- REAP must not rely on disclaimers to override CGA protections
- Refund policy may offer better terms, but cannot exclude CGA rights for consumers in NZ

## Operational Recommendations

### Terms & Conditions
- Add clear CGA and Fair Trading compliance statements
- State that participant rights are not reduced by the agreement
- Provide an accessible dispute resolution process

### Refund Process
- Document refund timings (5 business days)
- Maintain audit trail for all refunds
- Process voluntary exit requests promptly

### Marketing and Claims
- Review ads and landing pages for accuracy
- Avoid emotional language that suggests guaranteed wins
- Emphasize participant responsibility for meeting daily goals

## Conclusion
REAP can comply with CGA and Fair Trading obligations by maintaining transparent service delivery, clear refund terms, and accurate marketing. The product’s subscription model and prize structure are acceptable so long as consumer rights are respected and no misleading representations are made.
