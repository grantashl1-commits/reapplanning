# Data Retention Policy for Survive the Reap

**Prepared by:** Shelley  
**Date:** April 9, 2026  
**Status:** Draft

## Purpose
This policy defines retention periods for participant data collected by Survive the Reap (REAP) and ensures compliance with Privacy Act 2020 and internal data governance requirements.

## Data Categories and Retention Periods

| Data Type | Retention Period | Reason |
|-----------|------------------|--------|
| Participant personal details (name, email, address) | 7 years after account closure | Support billing, competitions, legal defence |
| Health and wearable data | 2 years after account closure | Operational analytics, dispute review | 
| Payment records and invoices | 7 years | Financial compliance |
| Refund records | 7 years | Audit and dispute resolution |
| Welfare incident reports | 7 years | Duty of care and legal compliance |
| Marketing consent records | 3 years after consent withdrawal | Proof of consent |
| Research consent records | 7 years | Regulatory compliance |

## Retention Review
- Quarterly review of data held in Supabase and supporting systems
- Delete or anonymise data once retention period expires
- Maintain a secure record of deletion actions

## Deletion Standards
- Personal identifiers removed for anonymised datasets
- Health and wearable logs erased using secure wipe procedures
- Backups purged in accordance with backup lifecycle policies

## Exceptions
- Legal holds override retention periods for active disputes or investigations
- Data required for statutory reporting may be kept longer if authorised by legal counsel

## Data Access
- Participants may request access, correction, or deletion of their personal data
- Access requests handled within 20 business days
- Deletion requests honoured unless retention is required by law or active dispute

## Governance
- Data Protection Officer: Shelley
- Systems owner: Ashleigh
- Review frequency: Annually or upon regulatory change

## Notes
- Wearable data is treated as sensitive data due to health context
- All processing is lawful, transparent, and purpose-limited
