## 2. Definitions

- **Season:** A 30-day period of participation during which you must complete daily movement targets in order to remain active.
- **Participant:** A person who has registered for REAP, paid the subscription fee, and accepted these Terms.
- **Alive / Active:** A participant who has met the daily movement requirement (21+ minutes at Zone 2 intensity or above, measured by heart rate monitoring device) or is protected by a declared Redemption Day.
- **Zone 2 Intensity:** As measured by a compatible heart rate monitoring device (e.g., Apple Watch, Garmin, Fitbit), Zone 2 corresponds to 50–70% of maximum heart rate. Behaviourally, Zone 2 is a conversational pace where speaking is possible but singing is not comfortable. Only activity at Zone 2 intensity or above counts toward the daily requirement; activity below Zone 2 does not.
- **Eliminated:** A participant who has not met the daily movement requirement and is no longer active in the current season.
- **Redemption Day:** A pre-declared rest day that protects a participant from elimination for one day.
- **Spot Prize Draw:** A promotional draw open to active, confirmed-paid survivors at predetermined season milestones.
- **Confirmed-paid survivor:** A participant whose subscription payment has been accepted and who is active in the season at the draw trigger time.

## 5. Daily Movement Requirement

1. Each day of the season, participants must complete at least 21 minutes of Zone 2 cardiovascular movement, as measured by a heart rate monitoring device or compatible wearable tracker.
2. **Zone 2 Intensity Definition:** Zone 2 is defined as 50–70% of maximum heart rate. Behaviourally, it is a sustained activity at conversational pace where speaking is possible but singing is not comfortable.
3. **Minutes That Count:** Only minutes spent at Zone 2 intensity or above count toward the daily 21-minute requirement. Activity below Zone 2 intensity (e.g., leisurely walking, light activity that does not elevate heart rate to Zone 2 threshold) does not count, even if it was recorded by a tracking device.
4. Activity must be recorded by a compatible connected wearable device or app (Apple Watch, Garmin, Fitbit, TERRA API, Strava, or equivalent heart rate monitoring service), or submitted manually through the REAP platform before 23:45 NZST on the same day.
5. **Heart Rate Verification:** Sport Waikato uses the heart rate data provided by compatible devices to verify that recorded activity meets the Zone 2 intensity threshold. Participants may be asked to provide heart rate export data or device logs as evidence in case of disputes.
6. If your device fails to sync, you must use the manual submission option before 23:45 NZST. Manual submissions are reviewed using participant-reported heart rate data or rate of perceived exertion to confirm Zone 2 compliance. Sport Waikato is not responsible for device malfunction or data loss.
7. Participants are responsible for ensuring their device is correctly paired, calibrated, and syncing with the REAP app. Sport Waikato does not warrant the accuracy of third-party wearable devices or APIs.

## 6. Elimination and Survival

1. At 23:59 NZST each day, REAP checks whether each active participant has met the daily movement requirement or declared a valid Redemption Day.
2. If the requirement is not met and no Redemption Day applies, the participant is eliminated from the current season.
3. Eliminated participants retain access to their account and may register for future seasons, but they are no longer active in the current season.
4. Eliminations are final for the current season unless Sport Waikato determines that a documented system error caused the elimination and elects to reinstate the participant.

## 7. Redemption Days

1. Participants are entitled to two Redemption Days per season.
2. Redemption Days must be declared before the season begins or at a time allowed by the platform rules.
3. A Redemption Day protects a participant from elimination for one day, regardless of whether the movement requirement is met.
4. Redemption Days are not transferable and cannot be banked beyond the current season.
