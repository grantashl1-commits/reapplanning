# Legal Sign-Off Checklist: Prize Structure for Survive the Reap

**Prepared by:** Shelley  
**Date:** April 9, 2026  
**Status:** Draft

## Objective
Document the legal sign-off steps confirming that REAP’s 7 × NZ$666 spot prize structure is lawful, promotional, and not a prohibited gambling activity.

## Legal Questions
- Does the spot prize structure qualify as a promotional competition under the Gambling Act 2003?
- Is the funding source clearly operational and not participant-funded prize pool?
- Are the prize draws incidental to the main app service?
- Is the prize value reasonable and not designed to unduly incentivise gambling behaviour?

## Evidence Required
1. Terms & Conditions confirming promotional competition status
2. Competition Rules documenting draw timing and eligibility
3. Privacy Policy describing data-driven elimination and prize eligibility
4. Refund Policy clarifying pro-rata refunds and voluntary exit
5. DIA enquiry response (if available)

## Compliance Checklist
- [ ] Prize schedule documented (Days 7, 13, 14, 21, 24, 28, 30)
- [ ] Total prize pool: NZ$4,662 per season
- [ ] Prize logistics funded by Sport Waikato operational budget
- [ ] Random draw process defined and auditable
- [ ] Winner notification and payment process documented
- [ ] Competition described as subsidiary promotional incentive, not lottery
- [ ] Eligibility criteria include active, confirmed-paid participants only
- [ ] No purchase requirement beyond subscription

## Sign-Off Notes
- Prize amount per draw (NZ$666) is modest relative to subscription and is distributed over seven events
- The primary purpose of REAP is daily movement challenge and wellness engagement
- Prize draws are an incidental reward mechanism and do not constitute the primary attraction
