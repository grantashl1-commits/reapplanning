# Gambling Act Safe Harbour Confirmation

**Prepared by:** Shelley  
**Date:** April 9, 2026  
**Status:** Draft

## Purpose
This document captures the legal analysis and evidence supporting the conclusion that Survive the Reap qualifies as a lawful promotional competition under the Gambling Act 2003 safe harbour provisions.

## Key Criteria for Promotional Competitions
- The prize does not exceed statutory limits
- Participation is not contingent on a chance-based entry fee beyond subscription
- The dominant purpose is not to win the prize, but to promote the service
- Prize draws are subsidiary to a broader promotion or activity

## REAP Analysis
- **Prize amount:** NZ$666 per draw, 7 draws, total NZ$4,662 per season
- **Participant requirement:** daily Zone 2 movement for wellness; not a pure chance-based contest
- **Entry fee:** monthly subscription to access the membership-based app
- **Prize draw mechanics:** random selection from active, confirmed-paid participants; transparent and audited
- **Dominant purpose:** health engagement, not gambling

## Conclusion
Based on the current structure and documentation, REAP aligns with promotional competition safe harbour principles. A final DIA or legal opinion should confirm the position once all materials are finalised.
