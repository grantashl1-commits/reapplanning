# Parental Consent Framework for Under-18 Participants

**Prepared by:** Shelley  
**Date:** April 9, 2026  
**Status:** Draft

## Purpose
This framework defines how Survive the Reap (REAP) manages parental consent, age verification, and protections for under-18 participants.

## Eligibility
- Minimum participant age: 18 years
- Under-18 participation is allowed only with verified parental or guardian consent
- Key exception: only participants aged 16–17 may join with parental approval and additional safeguarding measures

## Consent Requirements
- Parental consent must be explicit, freely given, and documented
- Consent opt-in checkbox must be separate from standard T&C acceptance
- The consent form must describe:
  - Type of data collected (heart rate, movement, health details)
  - How data is used in the game
  - Welfare monitoring and incident support
  - Refund and medical hold policies

## Verification Process
- Collect date of birth during registration
- Require parent/guardian name and email
- Send parental consent request email with a secure confirmation link
- Store consent evidence securely in Supabase records

## Risk Controls
- Daily welfare check-ins for under-18 participants
- Medical hold option available immediately
- No direct targeted marketing to under-18s via Facebook/Instagram
- Clear escalation path for any welfare concern

## Data Protection
- Treat parental consent data as sensitive personal information
- Retain consent records for at least 7 years after season end
- Provide withdrawal and access rights to both participant and parent

## Notes
- This framework is provisional and should be reviewed by legal counsel before enforcement
- Any under-18 participation must be consistent with the Privacy Act 2020 and local youth protection laws
