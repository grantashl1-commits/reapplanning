# REAP — Refund Policy
**Sport Waikato Incorporated | Legal Draft**

## 1. Overview

REAP is a 30-day daily movement survival game. The NZD $13.00 monthly subscription fee is consideration for access to the platform and the right to participate in a season. It is not payment for survival, elimination avoidance, or prize eligibility.

## 2. Before Season Start

1. If you cancel before the season begins, you may request a full refund for the current season.
2. Once the season has begun, the fee is non-refundable except as described below.

## 3. After Season Start

1. The subscription fee is non-refundable after the first elimination check at 23:59 NZST on Day 1.
2. Being eliminated from the season does not entitle you to a refund.
3. You may continue to access the REAP platform after elimination and may register for future seasons.

## 4. System Errors and Discretionary Refunds

1. If Sport Waikato confirms that your elimination was caused by a documented platform error, we may reinstate you and may offer a discretionary pro-rata refund or credit toward a future season.
2. Device failures, wearable app sync issues, or third-party service problems are not eligible for an automatic refund if no manual submission was provided before 23:45 NZST.
3. Any refund for a system error is at Sport Waikato's discretion and does not create a legal entitlement beyond your consumer rights.

## 5. Subscription Billing

1. Subscription billing is managed through Stripe.
2. You remain responsible for canceling your subscription if you do not wish to be charged for subsequent monthly periods.
3. Refunds are not issued for future subscription periods after the current season has started.

## 6. Consumer Guarantees and Fair Trading

1. These policy terms do not limit your rights under the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993 or the Fair Trading Act 1986.
2. If you believe Sport Waikato has breached consumer law, you may seek independent advice or contact the Commerce Commission.
