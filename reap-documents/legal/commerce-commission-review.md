# Commerce Commission Fair Trading Review for REAP

**Prepared by:** Shelley  
**Date:** April 9, 2026  
**Status:** Draft

## Purpose
This review evaluates Survive the Reap marketing materials and claims against the Commerce Commission fair trading guidelines and the Fair Trading Act 1986.

## Key Principles
- Claims must be truthful and not misleading
- Any qualifying conditions must be made clear
- Prices and refunds must be accurately represented
- Comparative claims must be evidence-based

## Review Areas
- Website and landing page copy
- Email marketing and registration messaging
- Social media ads and influencer posts
- Terms & Conditions and competition rules

## Findings
- Prize wording should clearly indicate the promotional nature of the competition
- Movement requirements must not be presented as guaranteed rewards
- Refund policy must be easy to locate and understand
- Any “guaranteed” language must be removed from launch materials

## Recommendations
- Add disclaimers where required
- Keep prize messaging factual and non-exploitative
- Ensure all ads include a link to the Terms & Conditions
- Record evidence of approvals for each campaign

## Conclusion
With these controls in place, REAP marketing can align with Commerce Commission expectations and reduce the risk of misleading or deceptive conduct claims.
