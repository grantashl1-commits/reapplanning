# REAP — Privacy Policy
**Sport Waikato Incorporated | Legal Draft**

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Sport Waikato Incorporated collects, uses, stores, and shares personal information in connection with REAP — Survive the Reap. It also explains your rights under the Privacy Act 2020 and how to contact us.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal identity
- name
- email address
- date of birth
- payment information (via Stripe)
- account username

### 2.2 Activity and device data
- daily movement records
- device source and sync timestamp
- wearable app data when connected
- manual submission entries
- redemption day declarations

### 2.3 Research and consent data
- consent selection for research participation
- consent selection for sponsor communications
- anonymised usage data for Living Lab analysis

### 2.4 Support and communications
- customer support messages
- email and notification preferences
- dispute submissions

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use information to:
- operate the REAP platform and process eliminations
- verify daily movement and administer season status
- run spot prize draws and notify winners
- manage subscription billing and refunds
- provide customer support and dispute resolution
- conduct Living Lab research and internal evaluation
- send service communications and safety notices

## 4. Legal Basis

Sport Waikato relies on the following legal bases:
- performance of a contract (to provide REAP services)
- consent (for research and sponsor communications)
- legitimate interests (to operate and improve the platform)
- compliance with legal obligations

## 5. Sharing and Disclosure

We may share your information with:
- Supabase, Stripe, Resend, and other service providers who support REAP operations
- external legal or regulatory bodies when required by law
- research partners in anonymised or aggregated form only
- relevant third parties when required to protect the rights, property, or safety of Sport Waikato, participants, or others

We do not sell your personal information.

## 6. Research and Anonymised Data

If you consent to research, Sport Waikato may use anonymised participant data for Living Lab analysis, reports, and academic publication. Identifiable information will not be shared outside Sport Waikato without separate consent.

## 7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as is necessary to provide REAP services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and support research. In general, we retain participant records for the duration of participation plus a reasonable period thereafter, subject to legal requirements.

## 8. Your Rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020, you may request:
- access to your personal information
- correction of inaccurate information
- deletion of information where lawful
- information about how your data is being used
- to withdraw consent for research or sponsor communications

To exercise your rights, contact us at [email address].

## 9. Security

We protect your personal information with reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards. We use industry-standard security practices for service providers, authentication, and data storage.

## 10. Updates to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. We will notify you of material changes and post the updated policy on the REAP website.

## 11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact Sport Waikato at [email address].
