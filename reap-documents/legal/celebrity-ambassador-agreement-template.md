# Celebrity Ambassador Agreement Template

**Prepared by:** Shelley  
**Date:** April 9, 2026  
**Status:** Draft

## Parties
- **Sport Waikato** ("Company")
- **Ambassador** ("Ambassador")

## Term
- Start date: [Date]
- End date: [Date] or until terminated
- Renewal by agreement only

## Scope of Services
- Promotion of REAP across ambassador channels
- Attendance at up to [X] launch or soft-launch events
- Use of agreed hashtags or campaign messaging
- Review and approval of marketing materials before publication

## Compensation
- Fixed fee: [Amount]
- Performance bonus: [Optional]
- Reimbursement of pre-approved expenses

## Intellectual Property
- Ambassador grants Company a worldwide, perpetual, royalty-free licence to use agreed content
- Ambassador retains moral rights and approves all content prior to publication
- Company may use Ambassador name, image, likeness for REAP promotion only

## Exclusivity
- Ambassador agrees not to promote competing fitness or prize-based challenge programs during the term
- Exclusivity scope limited to New Zealand and sport/fitness events only

## Confidentiality
- Ambassador must keep campaign details confidential until agreed public release
- Confidential information includes prize structure, participant acquisition strategy, and launch events

## Termination
- For convenience: 30 days written notice by either party
- For cause: immediate termination for breach of agreement
- Termination does not affect rights to accrued compensation or content already produced

## Warranties & Representations
- Ambassador confirms they have authority to enter the agreement
- Ambassador agrees all statements made on behalf of REAP will be truthful and compliant with NZ law

## Legal Compliance
- Ambassador agrees to comply with Advertising Standards Authority (ASA) requirements
- Any promotional messages referencing prizes or competition must not imply gambling

## Indemnity
- Ambassador indemnifies Company against claims arising from Ambassador content that is misleading or unlawful

## Governing Law
- New Zealand law applies
- Disputes resolved in Hamilton courts, to the extent permitted by law
