## 1. Executive Summary

### Purpose

This pack seeks formal Sport Waikato Board approval of the complete legal and compliance framework for **Survive the Reap**, a 30-day digital fitness survival game developed and operated by the Living Lab (LL) business unit of Sport Waikato. Season 1 is scheduled to launch on **1 November 2026**.

### What is Survive the Reap?

Survive the Reap is a subscription-based fitness accountability product. Participants pay NZD $13/month and must log a minimum of 21 continuous minutes of Zone 2 heart rate activity each calendar day or face automatic elimination at midnight NZST. The product is delivered via a web application at survivethereap.nz, built on React/TypeScript with Supabase (database and backend) and Stripe (payment processing). Heart rate data is ingested via the TERRA API, which connects consumer wearable devices. Season 1 targets a maximum of 500 participants.

### Why Board Approval is Required

The Board's approval is required because:

1. **Gambling Act 2003 interface.** The product incorporates prize draws for eliminated participants and surviving participants. Legal counsel (Shelley, external) has provided a written opinion (T012) confirming the prize draw structure qualifies for the "no-fee entry" safe harbour under the Gambling Act and does not constitute class 3 or class 4 gambling. The Board must formally accept this legal opinion and the residual regulatory risk.
2. **Financial exposure.** The prize pool, refund obligations, and subscription revenue are material to the Living Lab P&L. Board approval of the financial model is required per Sport Waikato's delegated financial authority policy.
3. **Reputational exposure.** The product involves public health claims (Zone 2 training), minor-exclusion obligations (18+ only), and celebrity/corporate ambassador agreements. The Board must formally authorise Sport Waikato's name and brand being used in these contexts.
4. **Data Privacy.** The product collects health biometric data (heart rate, exercise duration) which is sensitive personal information under the Privacy Act 2020. The Board must approve the Privacy Policy and data governance framework.
5. **Contractual authority.** The Celebrity Ambassador Agreements and Corporate Group Agreement template create binding obligations on behalf of Sport Waikato. Board-level authorisation is required to bind the organisation.

### Recommendation

The Living Lab team and management recommend that the Board:

- Formally resolve to approve all eight legal documents listed in Section 3.
- Accept the residual risks described in Section 5.
- Delegate operational authorities as described in Section 6.
- Authorise the CEO to execute all contracts post-approval subject to Shelley's written sign-off on each document.

## 5. Risk Attestation

The Board is asked to formally acknowledge and accept the following residual risks. These risks remain after all identified mitigation measures have been applied. Acceptance of these risks does not imply that management considers them likely to materialise; it confirms the Board has exercised informed judgement.

### Risk R-01: Gambling Act Regulatory Risk

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Risk description | The Gambling Commission or a court determines that the prize draw structure does not qualify for the "no-fee entry" safe harbour, causing Season 1 to constitute unlicensed gambling. |
| Likelihood | Low (Shelley's legal opinion supports safe harbour position) |
| Consequence | Severe (cease and desist, potential penalties, reputational damage) |
| Mitigation | Shelley's written T012 opinion; prize draw structure designed to avoid class 3/4 triggers; monitoring of Gambling Commission guidance updates |
| Residual risk level | Low-Medium |
| Board action | Formally accept. Direct management to monitor Gambling Commission guidance and report any adverse signals immediately. |

### Risk R-02: Participant Health Incident

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Risk description | A participant suffers a cardiac or musculoskeletal event during Zone 2 training and attributes it to REAP participation. |
| Likelihood | Very Low (Zone 2 is low-intensity; participants self-certify fitness) |
| Consequence | Moderate-High (liability claim, reputational damage) |
| Mitigation | Medical pre-participation disclaimer in Terms of Participation; 18+ age restriction; "consult your doctor" mandatory acknowledgement at registration; liability limitation clause |
| Residual risk level | Low |
| Board action | Formally accept. Confirm liability cap and disclaimer language is reviewed by Shelley. |

### Risk R-03: Data Breach — Biometric Health Data

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Risk description | Unauthorised access to participant health data (heart rate, exercise logs) due to a security vulnerability in the platform or third-party processors. |
| Likelihood | Low (Supabase Row-Level Security, Stripe PCI compliance, TERRA certified integrations) |
| Consequence | High (Privacy Commissioner notification required; participant notification required; reputational damage) |
| Mitigation | External penetration test (T041) prior to launch; Supabase RLS on all health data tables; Stripe handles all payment card data (no card data stored in Supabase); privacy-by-design architecture |
| Residual risk level | Low-Medium |
| Board action | Formally accept. Note that T041 penetration test must be passed before Season 1 launch. |

### Risk R-04: TERRA API Dependency Failure

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Risk description | TERRA API experiences an outage during Season 1, preventing participants from syncing valid activity data, causing wrongful eliminations. |
| Likelihood | Low-Medium (third-party SaaS dependency) |
| Consequence | Moderate (participant complaints, refund claims, reputational damage) |
| Mitigation | Season Rules include a "TERRA data dispute" resolution process; manual log fallback option; communication plan for TERRA outage |
| Residual risk level | Medium |
| Board action | Formally accept. Note manual log fallback must be operational at launch. |

### Risk R-05: Minor Participant Access

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Risk description | A person under 18 successfully registers, is eliminated, and seeks a refund or makes a complaint. |
| Likelihood | Very Low (DOB verification at registration; 18+ acknowledgement checkbox) |
| Consequence | Low-Moderate (refund obligation; reputational risk if media attention) |
| Mitigation | Date-of-birth field required at registration with automatic age gate; terms explicitly exclude under-18s |
| Residual risk level | Very Low |
| Board action | Formally accept. |
