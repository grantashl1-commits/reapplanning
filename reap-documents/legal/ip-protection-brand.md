**Prepared by:** Shelley

**Status:** Draft

## Purpose
This document outlines the intellectual property protection strategy for the Survive the Reap brand, including trademarks, logo protection, and domain rights.

## Brand Elements
- Name: Survive the Reap
- Logo and wordmark
- Tagline: [if applicable]
- Campaign visuals and graphics

## Protection Strategy
- Conduct a trademark clearance search for “Survive the Reap” and related marks
- Register the mark with IPONZ if the search is clear
- Secure domain names, social handles, and relevant digital assets
- Protect brand assets via copyright and design rights where possible

## Usage Guidelines
- Only use approved logo versions
- Do not distort or alter brand colours without approval
- Maintain consistency across marketing and partner materials

## Enforcement
- Monitor unauthorised use in digital and social channels
- Send cease and desist letters for infringing uses
- Document any third-party brand usage approvals

## Next Steps
- Complete trademark search
- File IPONZ application
- Register additional domains as required

## Notes
- If using celebrity ambassadors, ensure IP usage rights are expressly licensed
- Avoid third-party claims by documenting all asset licenses and author permissions
