## Clause A: Participant Wellbeing and Mental Health

### A.1 — Nature of the Product

REAP is a game designed to build daily movement habits through structured commitment and social accountability. It is a behavioural challenge product. It is not a mental health programme, a therapeutic intervention, a clinical service, or a replacement for professional health advice.

Participation in REAP involves daily elimination stakes, midnight processing of activity data, and notification of elimination status. These are features of the game. They are designed to create genuine consequence, which is the mechanism through which REAP drives behaviour change.

Sport Waikato acknowledges that for most participants, elimination is a game outcome — disappointing but manageable. For a small number of participants, in certain circumstances, an elimination notification may land during a period of personal difficulty and cause significant distress. We have designed the product and our welfare protocols with this possibility in mind. It does not change the product's design, but it does change how we respond when it happens.

### A.2 — Who Should Not Participate

Sport Waikato recommends that you do not register for a REAP season if you are currently experiencing:

- Acute mental health distress, including active episodes of clinical depression, generalised anxiety disorder, or suicidal ideation
- Active recovery from an eating disorder, particularly where structured exercise rules may conflict with clinical treatment plans or contribute to compulsive exercise patterns
- A period of acute grief, bereavement, or trauma within the last 30 days
- A medical condition for which daily moderate-intensity exercise is clinically contraindicated — please consult your GP if you are unsure
- Active treatment for, or recent history of, exercise-related injury where daily activity may cause re-injury

**This list is not exhaustive.** You are responsible for assessing your own physical and mental readiness to participate. If you are in any doubt, please speak with your GP or a registered health professional before registering.

Registering for and paying for a REAP season is confirmation that you have assessed your own readiness and have determined that participation is appropriate for you at this time.

### A.3 — Support Resources

If you are experiencing mental health difficulties during your REAP season, or if an elimination notification arrives at a time when you are vulnerable, please contact:

- **Lifeline:** 0800 543 354 (free, 24/7)
- **Need to Talk (1737):** Text or call 1737 (free, 24/7)
- **Mental Health Foundation:** mentalhealth.org.nz
- **Your GP** or local community mental health service

These services are free and available at any time. You do not need to be in crisis to use them. REAP elimination notifications will always include links to support resources.

### A.4 — Contacting Sport Waikato

If you contact Sport Waikato in distress following an elimination or at any point during the season, you will receive a human response — not an automated reply. Our welfare protocol ensures that communications expressing significant distress are escalated and responded to promptly and with care. Contact us at: [email address].

## Clause B: Gamification, Habit Formation, and Responsible Participation

### B.1 — Design Intent

REAP uses behavioural science mechanisms — specifically loss aversion and social accountability — to build a daily movement habit. These mechanisms are effective precisely because they create psychological stakes. That is their purpose.

Sport Waikato acknowledges that any mechanism powerful enough to change behaviour is powerful enough, in rare cases, to contribute to unhealthy patterns of engagement. We have designed the product with this risk in mind.

### B.2 — Signs of Unhealthy Participation

You should consider pausing your participation or cancelling your subscription if you notice any of the following:

- You are exercising through pain, injury, or illness in order to avoid elimination
- You are setting an alarm for 11:30pm or midnight to exercise, disrupting sleep
- You are experiencing significant distress (not ordinary disappointment) when you miss a session
- REAP participation is creating conflict in your personal relationships
- You feel unable to stop participating even when it is causing you harm
- You are prioritising REAP above your own physical recovery or medical advice

These patterns may indicate that the game is affecting your wellbeing in ways that go beyond the intended benefit. Please pause your subscription and speak with a healthcare professional.

### B.3 — Sport Waikato's Right to Intervene

Sport Waikato reserves the right to pause or terminate a participant's account, with a full refund of the current period's subscription, if we become aware of patterns suggesting a participant is engaging with REAP in ways that are harmful to their health or wellbeing. This is a welfare provision, not a punitive one. We will contact you before taking this action where it is safe and appropriate to do so.

## Clause C: Accessible Participation — Addressing Financial Barriers

### C.1 — Acknowledgement

Sport Waikato is a charitable trust with a mandate to improve physical activity across all communities in the Waikato. We acknowledge that a $13/month subscription creates a financial barrier for participants on low or fixed incomes — including many of the communities most affected by physical inactivity and its health consequences. This is a genuine tension we take seriously.

### C.2 — Pathways to Subsidised Participation

Sport Waikato is committed to ensuring that cost is not an absolute barrier to REAP participation. The following pathways are available or in development:

**Corporate and community group sponsorship:** Employers, health organisations, iwi, and community groups can sponsor group entries for their members or employees. The sponsoring organisation pays the subscription cost. Participants are not required to disclose financial need.

**Community scholarships (from Season 2):** Sport Waikato will allocate fully-funded scholarship places per season for participants referred through community health programmes, GP practices, or partner organisations. Applications are handled with discretion and without means testing beyond referral status.

**RST subsidies:** Regional Sport Trusts may negotiate subsidised group rates for community participants through their local distribution models.

**If you cannot afford the subscription fee** and believe REAP could benefit your health, please contact Sport Waikato at [email address]. We will do our best to find a pathway that works.

### C.3 — No Differential Treatment

Participants who access REAP through subsidised or scholarship pathways receive the same experience, prize eligibility, and support as full-subscription participants. Financial status is not disclosed to other participants, to group sponsors in a way that identifies individuals, or in any public-facing material.

## Clause D: Device and Technology Failure

### D.1 — Responsibility for Activity Tracking

REAP accepts activity data from compatible third-party applications and devices. Sport Waikato does not manufacture, supply, operate, or support any wearable device or fitness application. The reliability and accuracy of data from connected devices is the responsibility of the device manufacturer and application operator, not Sport Waikato.

**Under the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993 and the Fair Trading Act 1986**, Sport Waikato is not the supplier or manufacturer of your activity tracking device. Any complaint about device performance should be directed to the device manufacturer. Nothing in this clause limits any rights you have against the manufacturer of a defective product.

### D.2 — Manual Activity Submission

REAP provides a manual activity submission option as a backup for situations where your connected device or application fails to sync before midnight. **It is your responsibility to use manual submission if your device does not sync.** The manual submission option is available from the REAP app until 23:45 NZST each day. After 23:45, the elimination processing window begins and manual submission may not be available.

### D.3 — What Happens if Your Device Fails

| Situation | Outcome | Recourse |
|-----------|---------|---------|
| Device fails to sync before midnight | Elimination if no manual submission | Manual submission available until 23:45 |
| Device hardware failure (broken device) | Elimination if no manual submission | Disputes process (see below) |
| App sync error on a compatible application | Elimination if no manual submission | Disputes process — case-by-case |
| Device stolen or lost | Elimination if no manual submission | Disputes process — case-by-case |
| Connected app account suspended or deleted | Elimination if no manual submission | Not eligible for dispute — use a backup app |

### D.4 — Disputes Process for Device Failure

If you believe you completed 21+ minutes of qualifying activity on the day of your elimination and were eliminated as a result of a device or sync failure beyond your control, you may lodge a dispute within 48 hours of your elimination.

**What you will need to provide:**
- Written description of what happened
- Evidence of the device/app failure (screenshots, device settings, manufacturer support reference if available)
- Any alternative evidence of activity on the day (photos, timestamps, Redemption Day usage history)

**What Sport Waikato will do:**
- Review the dispute within 3 working days
- Check your activity records in the REAP database
- Make a decision based on the available evidence

**Outcomes:**
- If the evidence supports your claim and a system error caused the failure: reinstatement to the current season
- If the evidence supports your claim but the failure was in a third-party device/app (not REAP's system): Sport Waikato may offer priority Season 2 registration as a goodwill gesture, but is not obligated to reinstate you to the current season
- If the evidence does not support the claim: elimination stands

**Sport Waikato's decision on disputes is final.** The disputes process is a goodwill mechanism, not a legal right to reinstatement.

### D.5 — Season Entry Fee and Device Failure

A device failure does not entitle you to a refund of the current period's subscription fee. As described in the Refund Policy, the subscription fee is consideration for access to the REAP platform — not for guaranteed survival. Platform access continued during your season regardless of your device's status.

If Sport Waikato's own platform (the REAP app, the Supabase database, or the elimination processing system) causes your elimination through a documented system error, Sport Waikato will address this through reinstatement and/or a pro-rata refund at its discretion.

## Clause E: Vulnerable Participant Elimination — Ethical Position

### E.1 — Acknowledgment of Risk

Sport Waikato acknowledges plainly: there will be participants who are eliminated during periods of personal vulnerability. The probability is not zero. This is a foreseeable consequence of a product that delivers automated elimination notifications at 23:59 to people who are real human beings with real lives.

### E.2 — Our Commitment

Sport Waikato's response to this risk is not to disclaim it. It is to own it. We have:
- Designed elimination notifications to be honest and forward-looking, not triumphant or punishing
- Included support resource signposting in every elimination communication
- Created a documented welfare protocol for responding to participants in distress
- Recommended in these Terms that participants in acute mental health distress do not participate
- Committed to maintaining a human point of contact for welfare concerns throughout each season

### E.3 — Limitation of Liability

Notwithstanding the above, Sport Waikato is not liable for psychological distress, loss of enjoyment, or other non-physical harm arising from elimination from a REAP season, provided that Sport Waikato has followed its documented welfare protocol and the health recommendations in these Terms were clearly communicated before registration.

Nothing in this clause limits any rights you have under New Zealand consumer law. If you believe Sport Waikato's actions or omissions caused foreseeable harm that reasonable care would have prevented, you may seek legal advice about your options.
